# 2pc50 source-terminal operational reliability (station-only)

## Scope and definition

This is a read-only interpretation of the retained July 92-station, 318-edge, 14-Core-source model. It adds no failure model for the abstract edges, no capacity, voltage, generation dispatch, or load-flow calculation, and no second multiplier to the formal service trajectories. The 2pc50 PGA field is fixed. At station *i*, the **production five-state fragility probability kernel**, including its clipping and normalization, gives `r_i = P(DS0)+P(DS1) = P(F_i=1)` because residual functionality is 1.00, 0.50, 0.09, 0.04, 0.03 and the production threshold is 0.50. The 92 Bernoulli functional states are sampled independently, matching the fixed-PGA damage assumption. Each active Core source has its own `r_i` and is not assumed permanently operational.

For a non-source station, `R_path_full = P(at least one operational route to an active Core source | target functional)`, and `R_conn_full = r_i R_path_full`. Conditional reliability is estimated by holding the other 91 sampled states fixed and forcing the target functional in the connectivity query. This is a conditional Monte Carlo estimator, **not a new damage draw or a change to the formal Monte Carlo**. It avoids the near-zero denominator of filtering on an extremely rare functional target. An active source has conditional local-source reliability 1; its unconditional connection is its own `r_i`.

The most-reliable fixed source path minimizes the sum of `-log(r_j)` over its required nodes, **excluding the target and including the source endpoint**. Its exact independent-node probability is `R_best_path`. `Delta_R_redundancy = R_path_full - R_best_path` compares the full network with that single path. For each source separately, the same conditional query yields `R_to_source_s`; `Delta_R_source = R_any_source - max_s R_to_source_s` isolates the benefit of more than one possible source. The latter and total route gain are related and **must not be added**.

### Finite-sample precision and convergence

The new station-only analysis uses 100,000 states from `SeedSequence([20260926,3,991])`. Population-dependency-weighted **raw conditional** `R_path` at 10k, 25k, 50k, 100k states is 0.141857, 0.142074, 0.142160, 0.142014. The corresponding raw unconditional connected-mass index is 0.00096013, 0.00096281, 0.00096408, 0.00096138. The largest station binomial standard error at 100k is 0.000737; the largest station change from 50k to 100k is 0.00111. The aggregate is stable at this resolution, **but rare station tails are not**.

Here 41 of 92 stations have `r_i<0.01` and two have `r_i<0.0001`; the minimum is 1.215×10^-6. The previous 100k diagnostic filtered on sampled functional targets, giving a median of only 1,205 functional draws per station and zero for the rarest station. Its largest station `R_path` difference from the new conditional estimator is 0.00944 (GOODRICH: prior 0 versus new raw 0.00944, with only 154 old functional draws). This is an estimator-precision issue, not evidence of a changed topology or fragility law. The previous diagnostic also used the pre-audit DS2 shortcut; the exact five-state kernel is used here.

In 24 stations, the raw 100k conditional estimate falls below the **known exact best-path lower bound**, including 11 with no observed connection. The reported `R_path_full` is therefore `max(raw_MC, exact_best_path)`; both raw value/count and Wilson bounds are retained in the station table. This is a transparent **lower-bounded finite-MC estimate**, not an exact network reliability. Zero estimated redundancy at a lower-bound-limited station does *not* establish zero true redundancy. The empirical best-path event is a subset of the empirical full-network event on identical states in every station. Source-specific estimates are treated with the same analytic lower-bound rule. Claims about extremely low-reliability station/tract rankings require more targeted rare-event computation, not another interpretation of zeros.

### Static 2pc50 findings

The population-dependency-weighted conditional full-path estimate is 0.142032 versus 0.141215 for the exact best path, a lower-bounded alternative-route gain of 0.0008175. The corresponding source-diversity gain is 0.0007452; its overlap with alternative-route gain is substantial. Among the 78 non-source stations, unweighted mean best-path and lower-bounded full-path conditional reliabilities are 0.004786 and 0.005985, respectively. WILMINGTON (STATION C) has the largest estimated station-level alternative-route gain, 0.02597 (0.03171 best path versus 0.05768 full), followed by HINSON at 0.01202. These are probabilities of *source reachability given own functional state*, not delivered MW.

Through the unchanged M1 mapping, `R_tract = Σ W_ri R_conn_full,i` and the best-path reference is `Σ W_ri r_i R_best_path,i`. The population-weighted means across 2,315 tracts (9,066,522 represented people) are 0.00096172 and 0.00095081, a mean gain of 0.00001091 in expected connected dependency mass. The 5th/median/95th tract full-index quantiles are approximately 2.12×10^-10, 5.65×10^-5, and 0.00423. Bottom and top empirical 5% tails contain 415,326 and 447,776 people. Low-index tracts such as 06037134905 and nearby tracts are at or near the analytic floor, so their exact order is unresolved. The largest estimated mapped gains include 06037294110 (0.000551) and 06037294120 (0.000531). **This index is not the probability that an entire tract has electricity** and it does not use actual feeder/customer shares.

### Reliability during the frozen recovery process

For each of 1,000 frozen 2pc50 evaluations in each of nine distinct baseline cases (unconstrained plus eight scheduled policies), the code reads the saved `F(t)` and `C(t)` arrays at 0, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72, 120, 240, and 480 h. It tests the *fixed 2pc50 most-reliable initial path* on the **actual joint station state** in each realization; it never multiplies recovery-time station marginals. The reported dynamic conditional path probabilities use the observed functional count as denominator, whereas unconditional connected/best-path probabilities use all 1,000 samples. The path-operational event is asserted to be a subset of the frozen full-connectivity event.

Population-dependency-weighted unconditional full / fixed-best / redundancy-gain indices at 24 h are: Impact-first 0.2681 / 0.1309 / 0.1372; Hospital-first 0.2512 / 0.1261 / 0.1252; Centrality-first 0.2290 / 0.1000 / 0.1290; Vulnerability-first 0.2657 / 0.1241 / 0.1416. At 48 h they are 0.8489 / 0.6680 / 0.1809; 0.8334 / 0.6253 / 0.2081; 0.7896 / 0.6215 / 0.1681; and 0.8266 / 0.6201 / 0.2065, respectively. These values show that available alternative routes matter substantially **during recovery** even though the static initial-state gain is small in absolute terms. They do not rank the policies by community burden or establish a physical reliability standard. At 0 h every strategy shares the same index (0.000914 in the frozen 1,000 realizations); at 120 h the compared indices have converged to 1.

The production `e=fFC` already computes connectivity from each sampled state. Multiplying it again by `R_path` or `R_conn` would reuse the same fragility uncertainty and can double-count it. The probabilistic analysis explains what the binary gate means across an ensemble; it is not a replacement service equation. A surviving path in one realization means model-topological availability only, not capacity adequacy or actual delivered power.

## Reproducibility and source files

Run `python source_terminal_operational_reliability.py`; tests are in `test_source_terminal_operational_reliability.py`. The code reads exactly the following scientific input files: `Formal_Experiment_20260923/Stage 1 Output_expanded/physical_inputs_2pc50.npz`; `Data/Substations_PGA_IDW_CEC_expanded.csv`; `Data/substation_graph_CEC_edges_expanded.csv`; `Data/source_nodes_core_expanded.csv`; `Data/JULY_UTILITY_CONSTRAINED_92.csv`; `Revision_Mapping_Gate/SOURCE_NODE_EVIDENCE_CROSSWALK.csv`; and the 9,000 existing `2pc50__evaluation_0000.npz` through `...0999.npz` files under `Formal_Experiment_20260923/Formal_Trajectories/2pc50/C57_D1/{strategy}/` and `Formal_Experiment_20260923/Equity_Amendment/T/2pc50/C57_D1/` for Vulnerability-first. It also reads `SUBSTATION_CONNECTIVITY_RELIABILITY.csv` solely for comparison with the earlier 100k diagnostic. No physical sample, repair schedule, trajectory, mapping, or gate file is written.

The compact outputs are `SOURCE_TERMINAL_STATION_RELIABILITY_2PC50.csv`, `SOURCE_TERMINAL_TRACT_RELIABILITY_2PC50.csv`, `SOURCE_TERMINAL_MC_CONVERGENCE_2PC50.csv`, `SOURCE_TERMINAL_DYNAMIC_STATION_2PC50.csv`, and `SOURCE_TERMINAL_DYNAMIC_SUMMARY_2PC50.csv`. Station rows include all five DS probabilities, source role, mapped population dependency, intact route diagnostics, raw and lower-bounded MC reliability, and all 14 source-specific probabilities. The dynamic station table preserves functional counts to expose sparse conditional denominators.
