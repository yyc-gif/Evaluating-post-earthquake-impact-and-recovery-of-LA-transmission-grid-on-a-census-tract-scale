# July submission 92-station provenance: recoverable facts and gap

This is a read-only reconstruction of the July input. It does not substitute the later R1-310 inventory or infer an undocumented selection rule. Paths under `01_Source_Data` and `02_Preprocessing_Scripts` are relative to the parent `Project` directory; paths under `Data` are relative to the protected July-content reviewer worktree. The companion `JULY92_COUNTY_SOURCE_CROSSWALK.csv` lists all 558 Los Angeles County records in the retained merged source, with explicit membership in the 486-record snapshot, the independently reproduced 302-record city filter, and the submitted 92-record file.

## Why 92 differs from the separately reproduced 302

`02_Preprocessing_Scripts/extract_workinglist_substations_assign_fragility.py` applies `COUNTY=LOS ANGELES` and an enumerated list of city labels/aliases to `CA_substations_MERGED.csv`. Applied **in memory** to the retained input, that predicate yields 302 rows, without requiring `TYPE=SUBSTATION`, in-service status, a voltage threshold, or graph connectivity. The script's default output name is misleading: it does **not** reproduce the submitted 92. The 92 file also contains two `CITY_N` values for `LA CA?ADA FLINTRIDGE` that differ from this retained script's current `normalize_name` function, further preventing attribution of the exact 92 file to this script version.

Membership is: 73 in both; 19 in the 92 only; 229 in the 302 only. The 19 are all already in the 486 snapshot, all SCE-labelled SUBSTATION records, but their city labels are outside the helper script's accepted list (e.g. NORWALK, CERRITOS, MONTEREY PARK, INDUSTRY, PASADENA). Thus they were **not added from outside the 486**; the 302 city filter simply omits them. Their individual inclusion decisions in the 92 are undocumented. Their IDs are 300105, 300232, 300408, 300558, 300648, 300829, 301517, 301541, 302187, 302798, 303371, 304040, 305984, 306060, 306279, 306369, 307564, 307785 and 308907.

Of the 229 city-filtered records missing from the 92, 52 are non-SUBSTATION types (34 TAP, 14 RISER, 2 DEAD END, 2 unavailable-type) and 177 are SUBSTATION. Of those 177, 151 are marked `IN SERVICE`, 162 have a finite maximum voltage of at least 66 kV, and 19 have at least 230 kV. For example, `STATION P (MARKET)` (306012) is an in-service 230-kV LADWP substation in LOS ANGELES, yet is absent. Those attributes rule out explaining all 229 exclusions by type, status, voltage, or the helper's city list. Even the conjunction `TYPE=SUBSTATION`, maximum voltage ≥66 kV and non-IMAGERY source admits 331 of the 486 records: all 92 selected records **plus 239 unselected records**. None of these descriptive tests proves the original decision criterion.

One of the 302 city-filtered IDs, 308844 REDONDO 1, is absent even from the 486 snapshot, despite its Los Angeles County/REDONDO BEACH label. This illustrates that the retained 486 snapshot is not a complete execution of the separately retained 302 city predicate. Its omission is not documented.

## Recovery limit

The **extensional** selection is reproducible: use the 92-ID whitelist in the submitted file; all IDs can be traced through the 486 and 4,260-record sources. The **intensional** rule—why each candidate was chosen or excluded—is not recoverable from the retained evidence. Missing are the actual 558→486 geographic/feature selection operation, the 486→92 predicate or hand-selection ledger, the rationale for the 19 city-list exceptions and 177 otherwise plausible excluded substations, and provenance for the OLIVE owner edit. The later 92-node/318-edge topology is generated *from* the selected 92, so it cannot independently explain how those 92 were chosen. The defensible description is a retained, apparently curated 92-station model inventory derived from the 486-record county-area candidate snapshot, with a documented source identity but an undocumented final selection rule.
