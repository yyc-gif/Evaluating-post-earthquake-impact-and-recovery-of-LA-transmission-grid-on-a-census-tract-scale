# Review coverage and Fig06 scope

Fig06 compares absolute outcomes for 29 and 57 crews under 2pc50, with baseline repair duration held fixed. Q1-Q4 are vulnerability quartiles, not crew conditions. Panel C is mean realization-specific absolute Q4-Q1 separation in hours. It is neither a resource percentage change nor Vulnerability-first minus Hospital-first. No ambiguous percentage denominator is introduced. Its main-text placement remains an author decision.

The separate crew figure displays all four tested counts (29,57,86,114). The repair-duration figure displays all four multipliers (0.75,1,1.25,1.50) with 57 crews. Those are discrete tested scenarios, not an inferred continuous response.
