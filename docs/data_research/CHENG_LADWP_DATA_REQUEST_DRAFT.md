# Draft request: Cheng et al. 2024 LADWP transmission case

Subject: Request for an anonymized validation case from the Los Angeles earthquake-risk study

Dear Drs. Cheng, Nozick, Dobson, Davidson, Obiang, Dias, and Granados,

I am revising a study of post-earthquake recovery of a 92-station Los Angeles network abstraction. A reviewer asked us to distinguish topological source reachability from electrical adequacy. Your IEEE Access paper (DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3408797) describes a utility-provided LADWP case with 432 buses, 545 branches, transmission lines and transformers, substation demand, generator capacities, branch capacities, external injections, DC load flow, and load shedding.

Could you share, subject to any LADWP restrictions, the first feasible item below?

1. An anonymized bus/branch model used in the published study, including bus type/voltage, branch endpoints, reactance/base, status and normal/emergency rating, bus active-power load, generator/import bounds, and reference-bus treatment.
2. A reduced validation subset that preserves those fields and the published electrical calculation.
3. De-identified bus-load and branch-capacity tables plus a station/bus crosswalk.
4. If inputs cannot be shared, aggregate benchmark outputs for a supplied set of station-outage states: connected load, constrained served load, and load shed by station or anonymized service area.

We would use the material only for a bounded validation benchmark, keep the published network structure separate from our 92-node abstraction, comply with an NDA/data-use agreement, and avoid publishing sensitive facility identities or raw case data.
