# Publication-size diagnostic figure review

AUTHOR REVIEW — NOT FORMAL STAGE 7

All seven final vector PDFs (ten pages) were rendered at 150 dpi at their native 7.2-inch publication width and visually inspected after the final layout changes. PNG deliverables are 600 dpi. The underlying marks and values are produced by Matplotlib from verified frozen data; no generated bitmap, AI illustration or invented mechanism schematic is used.

- 01: Two original-unit scatter panels, colorbar and explanatory footnote are readable; no overlap.
- 02: Actual official tract internal points, fixed geographic aspect, readable continuous SD scale; no excluded geometry records.
- 03: Long policy labels fit after margin adjustment. Variance shares and leave-one-out changes use distinct scales and units.
- 04: WCSS and endpoint-chord plots distinguish the internal bend diagnostic from an approved K.
- 05: Full-cohort silhouette and seed ranges are shown. Logarithmic class-size axis makes 12-tract versus 34-38-tract classes legible; no observations are removed.
- 06, pages 1-3: Actual-unit K3/K4/K5 profile tables. Main titles and model headers have separate vertical space. Model-specific IDs, counts, population shares and mean/median definitions are explicit.
- 06, page 4: Six actual membership point maps. Map legends no longer overlap longitude labels; model-specific IDs are not represented as cross-model scientific equivalences.
- 07: Policy-set ARI values, labels and continuous 0-1 scale are readable. Removing Vulnerability is not duplicated as an independent test.

No clipped text, hidden classes, absent legend entries or invented source/network mechanisms were observed in the final rendered pages. PDF text-bound checks independently pass. This is visual presentation QA, not independent scientific validation.
