# Best qualified earthquake-inclusive candidate

**Recommend FEMA_NCV14_CA_v119 as the eighth-coordinate AUTHOR-REVIEW candidate**, subject to explicit approval of the14-hazard tract extension and California reference normalization. It has native frozen2020 identifiers, no missing/imputed selected inputs on2299 tracts, no direct social/monetary/loss adjustment, and direct earthquake plus non-earthquake content. This is not a faithful full Williams county-nine replication or an official FEMA score.

Columbia's actual data and metadata were retrieved and published sums exactly reproduced. It is weaker for this primary application: missing/no-hazard are conflated, no target has full14 component support, eleven2020 targets have missing donor land, and earthquake classes largely saturate. CDC CBM remains a useful published climate comparator but fails the new direct-earthquake inclusion requirement. Do not concatenate PGA and CBM to bypass either issue.

Local NCV variation is mainly heat wave33.73%, hail31.07% and strong wind24.71% by signed covariance attribution. Positive-share participation ratio is3.746; this is a contribution diagnostic, not proof of independent hazards. Earthquake supplies high baseline normalized score but little local membership influence. The coordinate adds a distinct geographic screening construct beyond first7 (conditional linear R2=.1817,VIF1.222); nonlinear random-fold R2=.6266 versus geographic-quadrant R2=-.2114 shows strong geographic co-occurrence and weak spatial transfer, not independence or causality.

**K4 is the strongest qualified review resolution within this registered NCV space**, not a formally adopted optimum: mean silhouette0.244426, seed-minARI0.973206, geographic-minARI0.591829. Normalized endpoint-chord bend for K1-6/7/8 favors [3, 4], while silhouette favors4. K3 remains a coarser defensible interpretation; K5 is notably sensitive to seeds, heat removal and regional normalization. The elbow is smooth/endpoint dependent rather than a sharp scientific threshold.

Policy-set LOO with cohort scaling has K4 minimumARI0.594182; fixed-main5 scaling gives0.587602. Multi-policy summaries are therefore not policy-neutral. Geography is only four registered median-axis blocks, not spatial external validation. K4 retains a38-tract initial-state exception containing all34 prior Q tracts; it is not a physically validated resilient community class. Mean initial service in that class is1.7306%, still near-total modeled outage. Removing Init with registered RQ1 redistribution changes K4 strongly (ARI=.29425), so this dependence is substantive.

All2299 positive-population tracts and9066522 residents are represented; eight positive-population zero-housing tracts remain and do not define the38-tract rare class. Original2315-tract study-wide outcomes, quartiles and formal Stage7 are untouched.

Equal input budgets do not guarantee equal realized separation and are not tuned to enforce it.
