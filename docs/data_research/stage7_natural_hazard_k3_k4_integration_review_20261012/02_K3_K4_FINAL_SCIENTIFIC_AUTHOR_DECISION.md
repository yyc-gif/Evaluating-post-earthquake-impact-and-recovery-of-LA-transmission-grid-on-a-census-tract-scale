# Recommend K3 primary; K4 sensitivity

This is a scientific-purpose decision within the qualified eight-variable NCV14/H2 reference, not a statistical claim that K3 is the universal optimum. No new fits were performed. K4 has better silhouette(.24443 versus.23374) and fewer negative silhouettes, and marginally stronger minimum geographic agreement(.59183 versus.57233). K3 does not win every numerical criterion. Elbow endpoints support3 or4 rather than a sharp unique bend.

## What K4 adds

K3 and K4 have label-invariantARI0.963225. The main new information is isolation of 38 of the 475-tract K3 parent group; all 38 come from that parent. The other primary patterns mostly remain:1234 of the 1242 high-SOVI parent remain together;578 of 582 higher-baseline-burden tracts remain together. This is a localized refinement, not four newly discovered recovery mechanisms.

The 38 group represents 136,453 residents(1.505% of cohort population), contains 34 prior Q-linked tracts, and has no positive-population zero-housing tract. Therefore it is not an artificial institutional-tract class. Prior frozen station evidence attributes97.19% of the 34 group's initial modeled availability to STATION Q(308581), a retained Core supply proxy. The new frozen-mapping trace of all 38 independently verifies34 positive Q dependencies and aQ contribution share of 91.22%. The additional four have zero Q weight and are chiefly served in the initial model by HAYNES(307373) and ALAMITOS(307039), other retained Core proxies. They must not be relabeled Q-dependent. This is linear post-processing of existing station initial means, not a new connectivity simulation. Source designation, its sampled operability, source gate and mapping are model premises, not independent validation of delivered electricity. Mean initial service of the 38 is1.7306%, still near-total loss. Init accounts for 78.70% of its centroid's squared displacement. Other raw-unit differences (lower unconstrained burden and grid impact; larger additional constraint burden) are real frozen-model patterns, but do not establish a distinct real-world restoration mechanism.

## Init sensitivity and substantive profiles

Registered removal also raises grid impact/HHI budgets from 1/9 to1/6. K3 ARI=.65536 versus K4 .29425. In K3,1236 of 1242 high-SOVI tracts remain together after removal; other recovery groups mix materially. Thus it is inaccurate to say the entire original typology persists without Init. The 38-tract K4 group disperses into three minus-Init groups(12/12/14); it is not independently recovered from the remaining coordinates. Presence and budget redistribution cannot be causally separated by these fits alone; algebraic distances and prior sensitivity disclose both.

K3 gives three regional joint patterns: C1 combines low spectral importance, moderate baseline deficit, low extra constraint burden, and relatively high SOVI; C2 combines lower baseline deficit with the highest average additional constraint burden; C3 combines higher spectral importance and the largest baseline deficit, with intermediate additional burden. HHI differences are modest, and all initial states are very low in absolute units. These descriptions do not imply actual electrical resilience, delivery adequacy or clinical recovery.

## Reason for primary resolution

Use K3 in the main integration candidate because it preserves the three broad disruption/recovery/vulnerability patterns without elevating a strongly source-premise-dependent exception to the same interpretive level as regional community types. Show K4 explicitly in S9 and the robustness figure to disclose the rare initial state and statistical preference for 4. This choice is based on source-grounded scientific interpretability and the intended community-level claim, not the number of RQs or resemblance to old figures. Author approval remains required; the official Stage7 and IDs stay untouched.
