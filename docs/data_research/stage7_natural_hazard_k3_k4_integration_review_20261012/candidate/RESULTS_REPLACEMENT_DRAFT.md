# Proposed community-typology Results

The eight-coordinate candidate partitions2299 positive-population tracts into three regional types. C1 contains 1242 tracts and56.27% of the represented population. It has low spectral network impact, an average Unconstrained deficit of 30.33 h, a comparatively small mean additional restoration-constraint deficit of 1.61 h, and the highest mean SOVI(77.05). C2 contains 475 tracts and20.93% of residents; it combines a lower average Unconstrained deficit(27.65 h) with the largest mean additional constrained deficit(6.96 h). C3 contains 582 tracts and22.80% of residents, with higher spectral impact and the largest average Unconstrained deficit(37.58 h), plus an intermediate additional deficit(4.15 h). The mean hazard-screening values are.6556,.5902 and.6201 for C1-C3, respectively; they do not follow a simple ranking of modeled earthquake recovery. All classes have very low absolute initial service. These are conditional model/type summaries, not actual customer-outage or electrical-capacity predictions.

C1 contains 407 of 579 frozen Q4 tracts and64 of 103 hospital-linked tracts, but its mean additional constraint burden is lower than the other candidate classes. Social vulnerability and modeled recovery burden therefore need not rank identically. This composition is descriptive: quartiles share the SOVI input, and hospital-linked location does not quantify clinical electricity dependence or restored service.

K4 has a higher mean silhouette(.2444 versus.2337) but mainly isolates38 tracts(136,453 residents) from the 475-tract C2 parent. Thirty-four are Q-associated, and approximately 78.7% of the rare group's centroid separation is attributable to Init_Supply. The average initial service is only 1.73%. This is a frozen-model source/gate/mapping exception, not independently verified resilience. K3/K4 agreement isARI=.9632;K4 is therefore shown as a sensitivity refinement rather than a separate four-mechanism regional conclusion. Registered Init deletion reassigns the rare group and materially changes other recovery classes; the ablation also redistributes R Q1 weights, as disclosed in S9.

The proposed separate co-occurrence screen uses frozen Q4 and aP75 cutoff of 38.3814 equivalent deficit hours. It flags94 tracts representing 350,799 residents. This is not a validated spatial hotspot or repair priority, and is not the previous four-term composite ranking. Author approval is required before this paragraph or map is adopted.
