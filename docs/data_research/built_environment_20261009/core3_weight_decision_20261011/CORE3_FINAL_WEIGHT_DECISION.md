# Final CORE3 reference-weight decision

Recommend **W1** as the reference convention for the frozen CORE3 candidate: recovery/service 0.20, grid dependency 0.20, physical CORE3 0.20, social vulnerability 0.20, monetary stock 0.10 and multi-hazard loss propensity 0.10. This is an explicitly chosen hierarchical representation convention, not an empirically optimal or uniquely identified set of scientific weights. It does not promote formal Stage7 labels or change any measurement.

## Answers to the six decision questions

1. **CORE3 should share one physical parent budget.** Coverage, residential configuration and classified-parcel diversity are distinct named subconstructs of community physical setting. Within that parent they receive equal one-third shares. This limits automatic extra influence from having three supported physical coordinates; it does not assert their measurement errors, populations or definitions are identical. No separate-physical-parent scheme was authorized in this experiment, so this hierarchy choice is conceptual, not a tested superiority claim.
2. **Keep Stock and ALR as separate measurements under one parent budget.** Call the parent economic exposure and expected loss. Monetary stock measures asset amount; unadjusted ALR measures loss propensity after exposure normalization and component aggregation. Neither duplicates the other, and low correlation does not determine their relative scientific importance. Their equal 0.10 split is the neutral declared reference within the shared 0.20 parent. W4/W5 retain the same parent but change its internal emphasis; W2 grants economic/loss information one-third of the total and therefore changes the joint-characterization question.
3. **Use W1, not a performance winner.** The research combines recovery/service, grid dependency, physical setting, social vulnerability and economic/loss context. Giving each parent 0.20 is transparent and avoids inherited column counts automatically dominating the distance. W3's larger grid and smaller social budget is defensible for a more network-focused question, but is not the balanced joint question here. W4's higher spatial median does not make ALR emphasis scientifically better, nor does W5's worse lower tail invalidate measured asset stock. W2 remains a substantive hierarchy sensitivity, not an automatic correction for Stock/ALR correlation.
4. **Reasonable weights materially affect membership.** Relative to same-seed W1, median aligned reassignment is 12.33% under W2, 34.50% under W3, 5.99% under W4 and 23.87% under W5. Raw class flows and original-unit means show genuine reorganizations, not merely a class-ID permutation. The taxonomy is specification-conditional.
5. **The four classes support qualified descriptions, not universal archetypes.** W1 optimization is highly reproducible (median seed-pair ARI 1.0000, minimum 0.9987). Its spatial ARI median is 0.8792, 5th percentile 0.7152, minimum 0.6929, with up to 255 aligned reassignments. Several major W1 profile directions persist under the registered deletions, but its 34-member class can expand to 92 and lose specificity. Other reasonable schemes reorganize major groups and can split that subgroup. Do not assign the small group a stable community-archetype name or insist that four groups are uniquely correct.
6. **Remaining limits:** weights are normative representation assumptions; k is fixed rather than newly validated; the 20 spatial cases contain 17 unique geographic deletion sets; measurement-support qualifications remain; four residential geometries remain unclassified; initial service and recovery are modeled inputs; clusters are descriptive and cannot attribute recovery causally to physical/social features. High seed agreement is optimization reproducibility, not independent validation. No extra weights, variables, k values or algorithms were searched to erase these weaknesses.

## Exact distance convention

After the unchanged full-cohort StandardScaler convention, squared coordinate weights in saved column order are [0.10,0.10,0.05,0.05,0.05,0.05,1/15,1/15,1/15,0.20,0.10,0.10]. Multiply standardized values by their square roots. B and Init split recovery equally; four grid descriptors split grid equally; three CORE3 coordinates split physical equally. Total squared-distance budget is one. The individual Stock/ALR measurements remain visible in all profiles.

## What this decision permits

Use W1 as the declared reference candidate and W2-W5 as the bounded hierarchy/emphasis sensitivities. Report major descriptions conditionally on this definition and carry the observed sensitivity into interpretation. This closes the weighting decision for the present candidate; it is not approval to overwrite formal Stage7, manuscript or figures. No additional model optimization is proposed or launched.
