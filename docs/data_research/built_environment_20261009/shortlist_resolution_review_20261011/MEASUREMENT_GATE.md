The eligible comparisons concern observed constructs, not physical causes of modeled recovery. Admission and preference do not use recovery correlation, cluster labels, silhouette or ARI. All valid zeros and uncertain ACS estimates remain. No universal coverage threshold is introduced.

- **Coverage:** EMC2022 is eligible for current built-surface occupancy. Its 100 m equal-area numeric product sums built surface from 2022 Sentinel-2 support, with explicit fractional tract/land intersections and NoData treatment. LARIAC is eligible as a source substitute with detailed vector boundaries but mixed older image dates. Neither has tract-specific ground-truth accuracy certification. Both are alternatives for one coverage coordinate, not two independent coordinates.
- **Residential configuration:** ACS5+ and detached-unit shares have actual positive denominators and verified 80-replicate uncertainty. These describe units, not building counts. They are different contrasts within a shared composition and only one enters a specification. Large MOE does not trigger deletion.
- **Composition:** SCAG2019 and SCAG2020 support classified-parcel composition only. The same eleven-class area entropy is eligible conditionally for that declared support. SCAG2020 has actual new numeric overlays, source origin, conflicts and coverage QA; 12.248% of county records are LU19 carry-forward and represent 36.806% of reported parcel ACRES, not clipped study land. Temporal substitution is a sensitivity, not independent validation.
- **Age:** Assessor2022 supports observed recorded Main Square Footage with valid actual year, not a complete all-use construction-year census or gross floor area. Condominium/parcel/building-line identities and exempt-stock completeness remain unresolved. CORE4 is a conditional extension and requires its own common-sample no-age comparator. - **Hardening:** official NLCD2022 C1.2 is eligible for buildings, roads and other impervious ground. HARDEN_EXT tests that explicitly different surface construct. It is not renamed building coverage.
- **Vertical form:** HEIGHT_HIST_EXT is a conditional historical roof-height extension, with valid-height coverage retained. Height is not stories, floor area or certified building volume. It is not a synchronized 2022 inventory.
- **EPA:** its historical household/job activity allocation has a different construct and unresolved within-donor spatial error. Keep the measured values and allocation stress tests as descriptive evidence; do not substitute it for parcel-area composition or fit EPA_ACTIVITY_EXT in this round.
