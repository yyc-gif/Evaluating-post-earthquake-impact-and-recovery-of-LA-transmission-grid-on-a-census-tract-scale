# Measurement admission rules, fixed before new aggregation

This audit tests measurement eligibility, not clustering performance. No clustering, PCA, new weighting, recovery simulation or GA is permitted.

All sources join to the saved 2,315 GEOIDs; the residential master remains 2,291. Geometry measures remain null for the four previously unresolved land-mask tracts. Their Census/FEMA/tabular values remain available. An absent observation is not a valid zero.

- **SCAG:** entropy is restricted to mutually exclusive, positively classified parcel land and uses a fixed 11-category normalization. Retain continuous classified-land coverage and ambiguous overlap. It is conditional historical composition, not complete 2022 land composition. Individual shares and entropy are alternative encodings of the same composition.
- **LARIAC:** use actual CODE=Building outlines, exact normalized-geometry deduplication, measured native units, and the established tract boundary/land mask. Union area measures roofline coverage; centroid assignment measures outline count. Size medians refer to complete outlines assigned by centroid, not clipped fragments. Retain source/imagery vintage and segmentation diagnostics. Missing/nonpositive/conflicting heights stay invalid. Positive heights alone do not validate every roof-height measurement; distributions and area coverage accompany conditional height candidates. No floor-count inference.
- **Assessor:** query RollYear=2022 only and a whitelist of nonpersonal attributes. Deduplicate by AIN plus roll year and audit conflicting records. Main square footage is not called footprint or certified gross floor area without a supporting dictionary. Separate single building-data-line records from multi-line summaries, condos and exempt/unknown uses. Years must be finite integers in [1700,2022]; actual and effective years remain distinct. Coordinates assign parcel records approximately, not individual building geometries. Aggregated main-area and age measures remain conditional/descriptive where building identities, scope or floor-area semantics are unresolved; no complete-all-building claim.
- **Raster:** official numeric product, exact year/version, valid zero vs NoData, georeferencing/units and fractional pixel-area intersection are required. Report actual valid land coverage; retain partial-domain means only with explicit support flags. Full-land means require no unexplained missing support beyond numerical intersection tolerance (1e-6 of land area). This numerical tolerance is not a remote-sensing accuracy threshold. Compare older mirror and official product on identical geometry. A product-level accuracy statement is not local tract validation.
- **ACS:** published estimates, positive housing-unit denominator, exact 80 aligned variance replicates and prescribed boundary model. Retain MOE/denominator flags continuously, rather than selecting only precise neighborhoods. Housing structure/age are residential unit measurements, not all-use building measurements.
- **EPA:** extensive household/jobs counts require a validated donor-boundary crosswalk. A naive GEOID prefix or averaging source entropy is not admitted.
- **FEMA:** exact archived v1.19 fields and IDs; published scores remain scores, monetary values remain monetary values. ALR is expected annual loss propensity, not pure hazard. Community-adjusted risk/rate fields are comparison-only when SOVI is retained separately.
- **Layout:** no extraction unless a distinct pre-event spatial-form construct and defensible boundary/dedup/node convention are established. Exclusion is documented; no selection by previous cluster separation.

ADMIT establishes a defensible candidate, not final inclusion or independence. CONDITIONAL limits its temporal/geographical/measurement scope. PENDING_DATA is never presented as available. No universal 85% gate is imposed. Continuous coverage and source-specific uncertainty remain in the delivered tables.
