Annual NLCD Data Access

Active
By Earth Resources Observation and Science (EROS) Center September 26, 2024
  • Overview
  • Data

Annual National Land Cover Database (NLCD) has a variety of options for users to download data from including EarthExplorer, web-based visualization tools, USGS ScienceBase, AWS Cloud, and Mosaic Download sites. Follow the links below to explore which platform works best for your needs.

Annual National Land Cover Database Collection 1 Science Products

The Annual NLCD conterminous United States (CONUS) product release contains six annual science products that cover the period beginning in 1985. Data products are available for download on EarthExplorer, the MRLC Web Viewer, the MRLC Mosaic Download website, ScienceBase, as Annual NLCD web services, and through USGS AWS S3 (Oregon us-west-2 region).

Annual NLCD Science Products Page

The six annual NLCD science products are available to download as conterminous United States (CONUS) mosaics beginning in 1985 through various data distribution methods. Below are more details on each of the data access methods listed above. 

MRLC NLCD Viewer 

The MRLC Viewer is a web-based visualization tool enables users to view and download Annual NLCD products, metadata, and animated graphics for a specified area. 

Download Tool

The MRLC Viewer Data Download Tool allows four different methods of downloading data: drawing a rectangle, drawing a polygon, uploading GeoJSON encoding, and uploading a singlepart feature only shapefile.                

EarthExplorer

Users have the option of downloading Annual NLCD indexed based on the Landsat Analysis Ready Data tiling scheme. The current Annual NLCD Collection 1 Science Products can be found under the Land Cover category, listed as Annual NLCD Collection 1.X.

MRLC Direct Download

Annual NLCD Collection 1 science products are available to download as conterminous United States (CONUS) beginning in 1985. Annual NLCD science products are available on the MRLC direct download site.  

Annual NLCD Web Map Service

Annual NLCD land use and land cover science products are available using the Annual NLCD Web Map Service (WMS) endpoint. Instructions for accessing the WMS endpoint are linked here: https://www.mrlc.gov/data-services-page

AWS Cloud Access

Annual NLCD data will be available via a requester pays USGS AWS S3 (Oregon us-west-2 region). Annual NLCD follows a nested directory structure as show below:

            s3://usgs-landcover/annual-nlcd/c1/v0/[region[cu-ak-hi]]/tile/h{xx}v{yy}/ 

            s3://usgs-landcover/annual-nlcd/c1/v0/[region[cu-ak-hi]]/mosaic/

ScienceBase

ScienceBase contains Annual NLCD Science Products and Reference and Validation data available here https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/655ceb8ad34ee4b6e05cc51a. 

MRLC NLCD EVA Tool

The Enhanced Visualization and Analysis (EVA) tool provides users with Annual NLCD science products at the county level between two different dates for easy and quick land cover change analysis.

Documents

Additional information on Annual NLCD and Annual NLCD science products can be found in the Science User Product Guide. The Science User Product Guide contains in-depth information about Annual NLCD including characteristics of the science products, algorithm description, and accuracy assessment.
