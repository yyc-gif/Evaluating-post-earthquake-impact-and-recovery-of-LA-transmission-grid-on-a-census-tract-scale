## P0011 / body block 11 | Front matter

Figure S6: Theoretical tract-level T80 recovery milestones under historical earthquake scenarios. Time required for each census tract to reach 80% recovered service functionality under the unconstrained baseline restoration model. Warmer colors indicate longer recovery times, while cooler colors indicate faster recovery. The spatial pattern of delayed recovery shifts across earthquake scenarios, reflecting scenario-specific damage intensity and network dependency structure. Color scales are panel-specific. Therefore, colors should be interpreted within each scenario rather than directly compared across panels.

Figure S9: K-means elbow plot for selecting the number of recovery-vulnerability clusters. Elbow plot showing the within-cluster distances across candidate values of k. The curve is used to identify the point at which adding more clusters provides diminishing improvement in compactness.


## P0022 / body block 23 | Front matter

Figure S10: PCA scree plot for the recovery-vulnerability feature space. Scree plot showing the explained variance ratio of each principal component derived from the standardized tract-level recovery-vulnerability features. The first five components satisfy the Kaiser criterion and together explain 69.5% of the total data variance. PCA is used here as a diagnostic tool for assessing feature structure and supporting visualization, while clustering is performed on the standardized original feature set.


## P0024 / body block 25 | Front matter

Figure S11: PCA loading structure of recovery-vulnerability indicators. Loadings for the principal components derived from tract-level recovery, vulnerability, and grid-dependency indicators. Strong loadings identify the indicators that contribute most to each latent dimension. Positive and negative signs indicate the direction of association with each principal component, while magnitude indicates contribution strength.


## P0026 / body block 27 | Front matter

Figure S12: Effect of IDW threshold on tract-dependency concentration. Change of tract-level supply concentration in response to change in IDW dependency threshold (θW). The blue axis reports mean number of supplying substations, and the red axis reports mean HHI. As θW increases from 0.01 to 0.05, the mean number of supplying substations per tract decreases, while the mean Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) increases.


## P0028 / body block 29 | Front matter

Figure S13: Strategy rank stability relative to the baseline configuration. Rank stability measured using Spearman’s rank correlation of restoration strategies based on population-weighted T80pop. Blank cells indicate parameter values that are not applicable to the corresponding sensitivity group. Most parameter groups show high rank stability; lower correlations occur mainly in cases where recovery times are nearly tied across strategies, so small numerical differences can change the ordering without implying large performance alterations.
