## P0069 / body block 70 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

4.4 System Recovery comparison


## P0070 / body block 71 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Each tract has its recovery trajectory (Si(t)) defined by its substations’ trajectories and the mapping matrix. The system recovery is the weighted sum of the individual recoveries.


## P0071 / body block 72 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Population-weighted system recovery curve:


## P0072 / body block 73 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Ssyspop(t)=i=1NPopij=1NPopj Si(t).


## P0073 / body block 74 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

SVI-weighted (population×SVI) system recovery curve:


## P0074 / body block 75 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Ssyssvi(t)=i=1NPopi SVIij=1NPopj SVIj Si(t).


## P0075 / body block 76 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

The difference in the two recovery curves indicate the efficacy of the restoration strategy in achieving an equitable recovery. Faster SVI weighted recovery is related to a trajectory more serving the interests of vulnerable population (See figure 20).
