4.3 Relationship to prior Los Angeles studies, historical context and applicability


## P0136 / body block 139 | 4.3 Relationship to prior Los Angeles studies, historical context and applicability

The revised results are compared with prior Los Angeles seismic power-risk and restoration studies by electrical representation, decision problem, and spatial allocation. Northridge milestones provide recovery-timescale context. The source gate is interpreted as a necessary topological availability condition, not sufficient electrical-delivery feasibility. Within the directly supported SCE subset, adding documented planning-capacity ceilings produces only small changes in the principal strategy comparisons; this does not establish adequacy for unsupported facilities, source supply, branch flow, voltage, dispatch or load shedding.
