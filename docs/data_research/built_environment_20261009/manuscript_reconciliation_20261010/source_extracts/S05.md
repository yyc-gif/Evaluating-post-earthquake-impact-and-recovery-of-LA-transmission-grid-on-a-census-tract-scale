## P0037 / body block 38 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Each tract has its recovery trajectory (Si(t)) defined by its substations’ trajectories and the mapping matrix. The system recovery is the weighted sum of the individual recoveries.


## P0038 / body block 39 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Population-weighted system recovery curve:


## P0039 / body block 40 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Ssyspop(t)=i=1NPopij=1NPopj Si(t).


## P0040 / body block 41 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

SVI-weighted (population×SVI) system recovery curve:


## P0041 / body block 42 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Ssyssvi(t)=i=1NPopi SVIij=1NPopj SVIj Si(t).


## P0042 / body block 43 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

The difference between the two above recovery curves indicate the efficacy of the restoration strategy in achieving an equitable recovery. Faster Social Vulnerability Index (SVI) weighted recovery is related to a trajectory more serving the interests of vulnerable population.


## P0044 / body block 45 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Key argument: Hospital-first secures fastest recovery, followed by the three GA schemes. Impact (population weighted or SVI weighted) could achieve higher speed comparable to hosptal-first in the first 20h, but remains similar to GA strategies in terms of complete recovery.

## P0061 / body block 62 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Cluster 1 (orange), which has the greatest share of tracts, represents the tracts with the lowest population density, lowest social vulnerability, and slow power network recovery speed. These tracts have low NRI score despite high building values and Expected Annual Loss (EAL), indicative of higher monetary values of assets in these tracts.


## P0063 / body block 64 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Cluster 2 (green) which has the least share of tracts and is concentrated on the eastern-central part of city, represents the tracts with the densest population, the highest social vulnerability, and high ratios of old housing. As these tracts have substations of the highest power network centrality and medium power network recovery speed, these tracts are likely suitable for substation repair priority.


## P0065 / body block 66 | 4.4 System Recovery comparison

Cluster 3 (red) represents tracts with highest NRI scores and high social vulnerability. Although these tracts have the fastest power network recovery, the residents in these tracts likely have limited resources to recover from hazards and may benefit from socioeconomic support.
