## Independent Results Not Invalidated By Clustering

- Formal physical samples, source gate, population mapping, scheduler and loss trajectories remain untouched.
- Fixed NRI-derived SOVI Q1-Q4 policy contrasts, including the paired vulnerability-first tradeoff in S19 Section3.4, are quartile analyses and retain their existing scientific scope. They are not new C1-C4 effects or income-group analyses.
- Original S19 Figures1-6 and Tables1-3 concern recovery, loss decomposition, fixed strategies, quartile burdens and resource conditions. Their payloads remain unchanged; the present task does not independently recertify every simulation statistic.
- A pre-existing standalone hotspot score could in principle be independent of cluster labels. That does not rescue old hotspot-by-cluster statistics or prove its current inputs remain valid. The exact score's source versions and supporting priorities require a separate audit before reuse.
- Station graph importance is a network descriptor, not a cluster-informed causal intervention or a measured electrical capacity. Geographic co-location does not show that repairing the plotted station caused the class's recovery burden.

## Unresolved Before Promotion

There is no approved replacement hotspot score, updated top10 membership, class-specific hospital burden, capacity strategy, or optimization sequence in this package. Author action is required to either remove those recommendations entirely or commission a separately scoped score/dependency revalidation. The old five-class conclusion cannot be repaired by changing a numeral, transferring old class names, or counting only matching members. The same34-member identity of old formal class5/newC2 does not preserve the old scientific interpretation.
