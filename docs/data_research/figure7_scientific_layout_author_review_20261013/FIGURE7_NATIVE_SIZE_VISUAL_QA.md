## Scientific story test

- The spectral-impact ECDF shows C1's pronounced near-zero concentration and C3's higher tail, whereas HHI curves overlap considerably. No type is claimed to be extreme in every feature.
- The paired recovery ECDFs and z-score profile show C2's lower Unconstrained burden but much larger constraint increment; C3's greater baseline deficit and intermediate increment are distinct.
- C1's SOVI distribution is shifted higher, yet its increment is lower. The NCV14 distributions overlap and do not define the same ordering as baseline deficit. The figure therefore does not equate vulnerability, hazard screening and modeled service loss.
- B clearly separates the three RQ groups, the sign of every deviation and the eight fitted coordinate names. The profile uses ordinary cohort z-score means, not independently rescaled local bars. Population is log1p population here, not density or original-unit population.
- The caption/QA provide exact resident counts and absolute means without turning A into a representation diagram. The source maps retain an absolute hour scale, and the Q4 outline is distinct from cluster colors and N/A.

## Statistical-scale warning

Large B z means are not per-coordinate percentages of distance. H2 gives equal *prescribed* RQ budgets, but realized between-class weighted SS is about 16.2% RQ1, 69.7% RQ2 and 14.1% RQ3 for these saved labels. NCV14 contributes about 3.0% of total between-class SS; HHI contributes about 0.06%, despite its registered budget. These diagnostics are not optimization or independent validation, and no weights or classes are changed. The full-precision QA table exposes both z means and square-root-budget-weighted means/SS.
