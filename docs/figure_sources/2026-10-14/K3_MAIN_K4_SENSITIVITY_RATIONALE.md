# P2 K3 main and K4 sensitivity

The saved seven-variable P2/K3 solution is the conditional main-text choice because its three broad classes describe contrasting initial/network, baseline recovery, added restoration-constraint and social/population profiles without making a rare Core-source-proxy state a separate general community type. This is a parsimony and interpretation choice, not statistical superiority. K4 must remain as sensitivity: its minimum held-out geographic ARI is 0.805674, compared with 0.418931 for K3, and its median full-cohort geographic agreement is also higher. The coarse geographic leave-outs are conditional robustness checks, not independent physical validation. Both classifications depend on the frozen earthquake scenario, source assumptions, sparse Init_Supply coordinate and finite five-policy comparison set. NCV14 remains supplementary environmental-hazard context, not a P2 coordinate. No new clustering was performed for this local publication check.
