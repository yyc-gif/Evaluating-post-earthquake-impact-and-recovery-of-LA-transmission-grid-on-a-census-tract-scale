- Figure 1 retains its five-stage composition. Stages 1-3 are pixel-identical to their accepted source. Priority ranks, parallel crew histories and actual completion/source/tract states are legible; arbitrary worker icons and invented tract cells are absent. No recovery-outcome curve is inserted into Stage 4.

- The schedule preserves twelve actual tasks on five of 57 crews and the 0-60 h axis. Travel intervals are short relative to repair durations and remain narrow at this honest common time scale; their widths are not artificially inflated. The gray/teal legend distinguishes travel and repair. This is an execution overview, not a detailed task-identification chart.
- Figure 1 is 185 x 265.289 mm; Stage 4 lettering is at least 7.5 pt. Across the sixteen figures, the smallest extracted nonempty text is 7.0 pt. All extracted fonts are embedded Arial regular/bold. No extracted text crosses a page boundary. Visual review found no overlapping labels or clipped geography.
- Complete-composition centering preserves all panel sizes and aspect ratios. Final left/right visible ink margins are approximately 8.0/7.8 mm (Figure 7), 17.8/17.6 mm (S2), and 13.9/13.7 mm (S7). These are measurements, not invented journal requirements.
