# Potential journals for advisor discussion — 2026-09-29

This is a discussion list only. It does not rank journals or select a target.
The “possible concern” column is an editorial-fit inference from each journal’s
published scope, not a statement about editorial policy.

| Journal | Publisher | Scope overlap | Framing it would favor | Possible concern |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Journal of Infrastructure Systems | American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) | Interdisciplinary infrastructure systems, including energy distribution, management, resilience, uncertainty, and social equity. [Official aims and scope](https://ascelibrary.org/page/jitse4/editorialboard) | Lead with the transmission system as an interconnected civil infrastructure system, the engineering method, and implications for system management and recovery. | The paper should make its engineering contribution explicit and bound what a topology-based service gate can establish. |
| Natural Hazards Review | American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) | Natural-hazard loss reduction and the interactions among hazards, the built environment, and people, integrating engineering and social science. [Official mission and scope](https://ascelibrary.org/doi/10.1061/%28ASCE%291527-6988%282008%299%3A4%28169%29) | Lead with earthquake impact, restoration, distributional community outcomes, and long-term disaster resilience. | The hazard and loss-reduction contribution would need to remain central, rather than presenting the work primarily as a grid algorithm comparison. |
| Sustainable Cities and Society | Elsevier | Urban resilience, energy distribution, smart grids, critical infrastructure, social impacts, and decision support for cities. [Official journal description and scope](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/sustainable-cities-and-society/2210-6707) | Emphasize continuity of urban energy services and how recovery choices distribute outcomes across census tracts. | A single metropolitan case may need a clear transferable lesson for sustainable or socially resilient cities. |
| Sustainable and Resilient Infrastructure | Taylor & Francis | Resilience of physical and non-physical infrastructure under natural and human-caused hazards, including social, economic, and institutional context. [Official aims and scope](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/tsri20) | Emphasize infrastructure recovery together with community context and broad resilience implications. | The case study should communicate implications beyond the local grid and connect the analysis to wider infrastructure resilience. |
| Reliability Engineering & System Safety | Elsevier, published with the European Safety and Reliability Association | Reliability and safety methods for complex technological systems, uncertainty, and system impacts of earthquakes and other hazards. [Official journal description and scope](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/reliability-engineering-and-system-safety/0951-8320) | Lead with the reliability/recovery formulation, uncertainty treatment, and a clearly bounded system-level engineering contribution. | The current model does not include power-flow adequacy, line loading, or capacity constraints; a reliability-methods framing should state that boundary plainly. |
| International Journal of Critical Infrastructure Protection | Elsevier; official journal of IFIP Working Group 11.10 on Critical Infrastructure Protection | Protection and continuity of critical infrastructure, including energy, dependencies, and practical engineering, economic, and policy approaches. [Official journal description and scope](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/international-journal-of-critical-infrastructure-protection/1874-5482) | Emphasize post-earthquake continuity of critical energy infrastructure and how restoration priorities affect communities. | The paper does not model cross-sector dependencies, cyber protection, or a protection intervention, so the protection contribution would need careful framing. |
