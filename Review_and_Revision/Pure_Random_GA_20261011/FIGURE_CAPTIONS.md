# FIG_GA_RECOVERY

Mean population-weighted effective-service availability on 2,000 new shared realizations. Panel A expands the first96 h; panel B retains the full 0-480 h horizon. Blue is the frozen random-only GA, orange Impact-first. These are means of realized event trajectories, not Hazus mean curves or actual delivered MW. The average per-realization T80 is not the 80% crossing of this mean curve.

# FIG_GA_POPULATION_LOSS

Normalized population service loss under the fixed two main policies. Panel A shows policy means and descriptive pointwise 95% bootstrap intervals. Panel B shows the full empirical CDF of paired GA-minus-Impact-first losses; negative differences favor GA. The confirmatory population comparison uses the separate simultaneous two-contrast interval in the result table, not the marginal policy-mean intervals in panel A.

# FIG_GA_HOSPITAL_LOSS

Unweighted mean normalized burden over 103 hospital-associated tracts. Panel A shows policy means and pointwise 95% bootstrap intervals; panel B the paired mean difference and pointwise 95% interval. This measures a tract association proxy, not facility load, patients, hospital uptime or energy loss.

# FIG_GA_EQUITY

Population-weighted burden within the frozen SOVI tract quartiles (Q1 lowest, Q4 highest vulnerability), plus paired differences in mean per-realization absolute Q4-Q1 separation and population-weighted burden Gini. All intervals are descriptive pointwise 95%. Lower group burdens coexist with higher disparity measures. Quartile groups are not equal-population bins, and mean absolute separation is not the absolute mean signed gap.
