# GA methods replacement for author review

A station-priority permutation was optimized using a genetic algorithm with a population of 100 independently randomized complete 92-station permutations, ordered crossover with probability 0.80, swap mutation with probability 0.10, tournament selection of size 3, and one generational elite. No heuristic sequence or previously optimized solution was inserted into the initialization or archive. A passive best-observed archive retained the best chromosome actually evaluated. There was no adaptive mutation, restart, or diversity-triggered replacement.

Twenty independent runs used seeds 2026103100-2026103119, each with a separate cache and a budget of 100,000 distinct evaluations. Cached duplicates did not consume this budget. Fitness was the unchanged negative mean normalized population-weighted service-loss integral over the original 64 planning realizations. The original physical model, repair-duration samples, dispatch scheduler, source-connectivity gate, directed travel matrices, and tract dependency mapping were held fixed.

The final permutation was the minimum-loss final chromosome across these 20 runs. Losses within 1e-9 equivalent full-service hours of the minimum were resolved by the lowest run seed; this rule did not affect the observed selection because the minimum was unique. No additional searches were performed to choose the reported candidate, and longer-budget sensitivity continuations were excluded from candidate selection. The selected run was seed 2026103108, with a planning loss of 32.99552241352315 h. Its complete ordered permutation has SHA-256 edc4ad19f61a6d89d83532c1c92f353243b5d9e7b475c1ad99873227bbfeb99a, using UTF-8 station IDs separated by newlines and a trailing newline.

The random-only initialization specification was a data-supported post-data methodological choice, not a successful application of the original automatic adoption criterion. At the selected 100,000-evaluation budget, 10 of 20 individual random-only runs met the strict proximity cutoff of 0.001 h above the previously frozen planning best; this did not satisfy the prespecified 90% criterion. Thus, the procedure uses repeated independent runs and does not imply that every individual run reliably reaches the same quality or that global optimality has been demonstrated.

## Completed Candidate-Specific Validation

The candidate, comparator sequences and paired analysis were frozen and pushed before any candidate-specific fresh outcomes were generated. Following explicit human authorization, 2,000 new independent shared damage/repair realizations were drawn under the unchanged 2pc50 field, with sampling seed 2026110300 and independent per-realization SeedSequences. None overlapped the 5,064 registered earlier physical states. Three fixed sequences were evaluated on each draw: the final GA, Impact-first and the unchanged legacy formal GA, retained as a supplementary provenance comparator. The 480 h horizon, original model, objective, scheduler and source gate were unchanged. No optimizer search, tuning or reselection was performed during validation.

All 6,000 production trajectories passed independent kernel parity and saved event-integral checks. Paired inference used 20,000 bootstrap resamples of whole realizations, seed 2026110400. The two population-loss contrasts have simultaneous 95% Bonferroni percentile intervals. Hospital-associated tract burden, quartile burdens, gap, Gini and system T80 intervals are descriptive pointwise 95%. Hospital burden is an unweighted mean over 103 associated tracts; SOVI groups are tract quartiles, not equal-population bins, with population weighting within groups. Absolute gap and Gini are calculated for each realization before averaging. The previously completed legacy validation remains separate and is not attributed to the new candidate.
