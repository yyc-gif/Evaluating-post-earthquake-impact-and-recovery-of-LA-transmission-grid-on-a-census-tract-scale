# Prospective GA results

On 2,000 new paired physical realizations under 2pc50/C57_D1/M1/G1, mean population-weighted normalized service loss was 33.048 h for GA and 33.581 h for Impact-first. The paired GA-minus-Impact-first difference was -0.533 h (simultaneous 95% interval [-0.563982, -0.502321] h), a 1.59% reduction. GA had lower loss in 1741 realizations and higher loss in 259. Reduced source-path loss (-0.821 h) offset increases in self -loss (0.277 h) and threshold loss (0.011 h). Figure 1 displays the new-cohort mean effective-service curves; no legacy GA curve is relabeled.

Mean normalized burden over hospital-associated tracts was 32.826 h for GA and 33.087 h for Impact-first. The paired difference was -0.261 h (pointwise 95% interval [-0.288579, -0.233509] h). This unweighted tract proxy does not measure hospital load, patient disruption or delivered power. System T80 averaged45.179 h and 45.273 h, respectively; none was censored at 480 h.

The aggregate improvement did not reduce dispersion. GA lowered population-weighted burden in every frozen SOVI tract quartile, with a larger reduction in Q4 than Q1. However, because Q4's baseline burden was already lower, mean absolute Q4-Q1 separation increased by 0.233 h (pointwise 95% interval [0.206896, 0.258975] h), and population-weighted burden Gini increased by 0.00979 (pointwise 95% interval [0.009298, 0.010293]). Figure 4 and Table GA1 therefore report absolute group burdens and disparity separately. These results do not establish an equity improvement or a universal realization-level advantage.
