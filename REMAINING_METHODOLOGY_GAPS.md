# Remaining methodology boundaries after the 2pc50 reliability analysis

## Scientifically resolved before this turn

The revision's production M1 utility-compatible mapping, paired realization-specific damage/duration scheduling, single completion-event recovery clock, 14-Core-source threshold-0.5 gate, direct community-burden GA planning, distributional metrics, and Stage 7 SOVI harmonization are unchanged here. The protected July submission remains at `182686868cffe962739804f6bc0ccecaed73d601`. The fixed 2pc50 station PGA and formal evaluation physical samples are the authority for this turn; the historical hazards remain contextual and are not redesigned.

## Solved by this turn

The station's operational probability is now explicitly `P(DS0)+P(DS1)` from the **same five-state production fragility probability kernel** rather than a shortcut through only DS2 exceedance. Conditional full-network source reachability accounts for probabilistic failures of *other* stations and of the Core source endpoints. The exact most-reliable single path supplies a comparator; separate any-source and individual-source estimates distinguish alternative routes from source diversity. A population-weighted M1 tract index shows how those station probabilities enter the existing dependency proxy. The frozen 2pc50 recovery trajectories show how the full-versus-fixed-path gap evolves with policy without redrawing or independently multiplying station probabilities during recovery. This is an interpretation/validation analysis of the existing gate, not a new fragility, capacity, or delivered-power model.

## Unresolved but bounded limitations

**Static reliability tails.** A 100k Monte Carlo resolves the population-weighted aggregate much better than very rare station-specific paths. Twenty-four *raw* full-network frequencies fall below the analytically known best-path probability. The adopted common-event decomposition uses the exact path probability plus a nonnegative same-draw residual and does not clip an estimator. Thirty-one station residuals have zero observed extra-route events; this is not proof their true gains are zero. The released table retains raw frequencies/counts and residual-event uncertainty. A targeted rare-event or exact network reliability calculation would be needed for precise tail ranking, without altering production results.

**Edge failure and electrical adequacy.** The retained 318 edges are abstract connectivity links. The existing external-data work contains circuit geometry and some named station/facility planning-load context, but no defensible edge-specific earthquake failure probabilities aligned to all 318 abstract links. It also does not provide a complete, compatible set of edge impedance, thermal ratings, bus injections, source/import capacities, dispatch and loads for this 92-node graph. Accordingly no `p_e`, line-flow, voltage, power balance, or load-shedding calculation is added. The bounded check already supported is same-facility local planning loading, not a network capacity model: the retained SCE GNA screen matches 33 July names/44 voltage-level facilities, with 34 internally screenable 2026 rows. OLINDA 66/12 has planning demand 28.45 against facility limit 26.09 (ratio 1.09046; provider loading 109.04%; margin -2.36 in the source's native units). This is a **planning facility** constraint context, not observed post-earthquake overload or a rating for a July abstract edge. ICA hosting capacity is also not earthquake deliverable MW. A minimal future physical extension would require a separately sourced, facility-matched validation of loading/rating definitions; it should remain local and not populate the 318 abstract edges. A real DC/AC benchmark requires a separate genuine bus/branch case.

**Source identity and adequacy.** `Revision_Mapping_Gate/SOURCE_NODE_EVIDENCE_CROSSWALK.csv` classifies six of the 14 retained Core sources as import/interface proxies and eight as in-basin generation proxies. The names cover documented complexes or stations, but the file confirms **no independent injection or availability for any modeled source**. For example, Sylmar East/West share an import complex without an individual bus/converter crosswalk; Station Q (Harbor) is documented as a receiving/distribution station rather than an identified generating unit. Each source still receives the same station-specific fragility treatment as every other modeled station. Source reachability therefore represents assumed upstream availability points, not generation capacity or adequacy. A meaningful future source-adequacy check requires station-to-bus/plant/import crosswalk, time-specific available generation/import limits, and associated demand/dispatch. None is imputed here.

**Existing physical uncertainty boundary.** The formal DS sampler is conditional on fixed scenario PGA, with independent per-station damage draws and no sampled spatial ground-motion residual, fragility-parameter epistemic distribution, or independent edge failure. These boundaries predate this turn and are not caused by the reliability analysis. Recovery-time functional states are dependent through the shared crew schedule; the dynamic calculation uses their saved joint realization states directly.

## Genuine author decision or new-data need

**Repair-duration provenance and interpretation.** `C257H_Project_Main.py` retains positive-normal task-duration parameters DS1 `(1,0.5)`, DS2 `(6,3)`, DS3 `(12,4)`, DS4 `(36,12)` hours. Their comment text first appears in the May 20 initial project upload (`df1fc85`); no source for the exact tuples was recovered from the retained code/notes. The DS2 comment explicitly decomposes its mean into “travel/inspection (3.5 h) + isolate one phase (2.5 h)” and cites a generic “standard utility operating protocol for N-1 operation,” while the current scheduler separately adds directed road travel. That wording creates a plausible **parameter-definition overlap**; the files do not establish whether the 3.5 h was travel to site, on-site inspection, or an obsolete informal rationale for an assumed 6 h task. The numerical tuples should be identified as adopted **service-restoration scenario assumptions** until the author can document their physical definitions; they must not be attributed to HAZUS substation equipment-replacement times. Resolving whether to reinterpret or revise DS2 requires source evidence and a scientific decision, and would require a new formal run if the numbers change. This turn leaves them untouched.

**92-node selection rule.** The retained lineage in `July92_Provenance/JULY92_SELECTION_TRACE.md` and its county crosswalk traces the 92 unique IDs to the 486-record candidate snapshot, which is itself sourced from the merged CEC/HIFLD California substation inventory (4,260 records; 558 Los Angeles County). The submitted 92 and 486 tables are already present in the May 20 initial Git upload and unchanged at July submission. The extensional 92-ID whitelist and 318-edge adjacency are reproducible. The exact 558→486 operation and **486→92 selection predicate/hand-selection ledger are not retained**. A separate city-label helper yields 302, overlapping the 92 in 73 IDs; 19 selected stations are outside that helper's city list, and 229 helper-selected records are not in the 92. Of those 229, 177 are substations, many in service/high voltage. Thus no type/status/voltage/city rule yet explains the submitted 92, and the graph built from the selected inventory cannot justify the selection retrospectively. The missing original decision ledger, not a 310-node replacement or an invented “486→92 electrical reduction,” is the provenance gap.

**Historical-versus-2pc50 fragility assumption.** The pre-existing July code uses `_old` (unanchored/standard Hazus-row match) fragility tuples for the three historical hazards and unsuffixed (anchored/seismic row match) tuples for 2pc50. `FRAGILITY_CONNECTIVITY_AUDIT.md` establishes the code and numerical row match, but the reason for assigning different anchorage assumptions by hazard and facility-level anchorage evidence remains unresolved. Cross-hazard differences cannot be attributed solely to PGA. Harmonization would be a new scientific choice and require a new run; none is made here.
