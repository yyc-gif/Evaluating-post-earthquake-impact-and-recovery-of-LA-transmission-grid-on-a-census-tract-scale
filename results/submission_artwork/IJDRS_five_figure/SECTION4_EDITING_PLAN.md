## Scientific flags by paragraph

- **P196**: The stated 337-tract denominator and claim that false assignments are prevented are not established by the current figure; verify the evaluation cohort and avoid treating assignment agreement as proof.
- **P198**: A hazard scenario is not an estimated 2% probability of a particular mean damage state. C partitions substation functionality, threshold and source-path loss; it does not model transmission-line damage. Check the claimed >85% disruption statistic and its unit.
- **P199**: Do not attribute spatial variation to topology alone: sampled damage, repair durations and dependency mapping also contribute. Source connection is not delivered electricity.
- **P201**: The manuscript's Figure 4 here is current master Figure 5, not the promoted source-connectivity Figure 4. Population-impact ranking is not a global optimization guarantee.
- **P202**: Do not equate targeted benefits or a small overall mean penalty with a reduction of all equity gaps. Hospital-linked tract outcomes are not hospital operating performance.
- **P204**: Q4 is highest SOVI, not a poverty-only group. Equivalent service-deficit hours are not outage duration. A small aggregate penalty does not by itself establish equity.
- **P205**: These are social-vulnerability quartiles, not income quartiles. Check signed separation versus absolute separation before calling a gap smaller.
- **P206**: Interpret changes as integrated equivalent deficit, not a change in repair duration or a guaranteed time-to-recovery improvement.
- **P207**: The empirical realization ranges span zero in the displayed Gini contrasts. Do not turn a mean reduction into a definitive significance claim or label all reference strategies equitable.
- **P208**: The +/-20 h map is a cumulative service-deficit difference, not restoration 20 hours earlier/later. Preserve the scale but correct that interpretation in a later authorized text edit.
- **P209**: Differences are equivalent deficit hours; 'recovery earlier' would require a recovery-time comparison. Whiskers here are bootstrap confidence intervals for mean effects, unlike Figure 3 realization ranges.
- **P210**: A crew-abundance result does not establish that priority selection matters only under scarcity in every scenario or metric.
- **P211**: Panels show change in mean within-realization absolute |Q4-Q1| gap, not the signed Q1-Q4 gap. SOVI groups are not rich/poor categories.
- **P212**: Hospital-linked tract service is not hospital demand or actual hospital functionality. Degree-first hospital-linked markers show means only; do not assign them unavailable confidence intervals.
- **P213**: Preserve the distinction between targeted-group benefit, total burden and disparity change; do not summarize all three as equity improvement.
- **P215**: Fitted-feature contrasts describe classes; they are not independent evidence that sharply separated natural community types exist. Geographic scatter alone does not validate clustering.
- **P216**: Smaller spectral impact and baseline deficit do not establish absence of severe physical damage or measure household coping resources directly.
- **P217**: Tract population is not population density, and lower SOVI is not a direct wealth measurement. Init_Supply's source-proxy/zero-heavy limitation must remain visible in the methods context.
- **P218**: The feature profile does not directly estimate repair intensity or demonstrate that grid damage takes longer to fix; use saved baseline deficit rather than a causal mechanism assertion.
- **P219**: The Q4/cohort-P75 rule is descriptive and policy-conditioned, not statistically significant hotspot detection or validated immediate-response priority. The five-policy set includes Vulnerability-first, which uses Q4.
- **P220**: Clustering does not establish causal physical damage versus geographic isolation or prove the root cause of inequality. These claims require evidence beyond within-fit profiles.
- **P221**: Lower population is not lower density. More intensive repairs and selection of the correct mitigation strategy are not demonstrated by these descriptive profiles alone.
