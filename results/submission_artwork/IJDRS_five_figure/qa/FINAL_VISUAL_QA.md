## Scientific equivalence

- Every selected chart/map is placed from vector source artwork at unit scale. No curves, estimates, uncertainty intervals, geometry or class memberships are recomputed.
- Fourteen non-legend crop comparisons have identical source/final word inventories and scale 1. Maximum mean absolute RGB difference is 0.289253 on the 0-255 scale; the maximum fraction of pixels differing by more than 32 is 0.00244618. These small rendering differences are not described as exact pixel identity.
- The workflow edit preserves all non-text form operators; only the strategy/class-key figure pointers change. Actual schedule labels and recorded event states remain intact.
- Resource/type PDFs and all eight original supplementary PDFs are exact byte copies of the previously verified optimized masters. The 32 accepted original PDF/PNG payloads match their frozen Git LFS hashes.
- Figure 3 uses realization ranges; Figure 4 uses bootstrap confidence intervals for mean effects. The captions explicitly distinguish these definitions. Hospital-linked tracts are not hospital demand or operational hospital service.
- Figure 5 retains K3 counts 1,235/428/636, sixteen N/A tracts and the unchanged Q4/high-deficit screen. No refitting, new composite index or hotspot-significance inference is introduced.

## Technical checks and limitations

All individual PNG/TIFF derivatives are caption-free, 600 dpi and losslessly equivalent in RGB pixels; TIFF uses LZW and an RGB profile. Vector PDF masters retain their source paths and embedded Arial typography. Review packets place each figure directly above its caption, on five, thirteen and eighteen pages respectively. A real root-level caption-font binding error found during inspection was corrected; regression coverage checks actual page resources and visible caption text.

All 75 binary outputs remained byte-identical after forced vector/packet reconstruction, and after the final normal rebuild. Hash-verified unchanged raster exports are reused rather than needlessly recompressed. The artwork-only command correctly records that it does not recheck a private manuscript.

The bivariate map cannot convey both SOVI and burden independently in grayscale. The existing aligned single-variable reference remains linked in the README. No claim of universal color-vision accessibility is made. No new scientific palette was introduced.

IJDRS five-main-figure guidance is addressed. Permission for thirteen supplementary figures, authenticated portal limits, final placement, table count and manuscript word count remain separate author/editorial gates. No maximum file size or figure height is invented, and no Word manuscript is labeled submission-ready.
