# Proposed caption: author review only

**Figure 7. Community recovery types and social vulnerability.**
(A) Six empirical feature distributions. (B) Cohort-standardized means of the
seven fitted variables. (C) Community types. (D) Continuous five-policy mean
cumulative modeled service deficit and the original SOVI score. Darkness
represents greater deficit; the blue-to-amber direction represents greater
social vulnerability. Outlines identify original Q4 tracts at or above the
study-area deficit P75, a descriptive screen rather than a composite risk score
or statistical hotspot test. White-filled tracts are N/A.

## Definitions retained outside the caption

The five priorities are Impact-first, Hospital-first, Degree-first,
Betweenness-first and Vulnerability-first. The displayed burden is the existing
Unconstrained deficit plus their equally weighted mean additional deficit,
integrated over 0-480 h and averaged over the 1,000 saved realizations. It is not an
averaged physically implemented repair schedule or an estimate of actual
delivered electricity. Random is not in this five-policy mean. Original
vulnerability quartiles are used only for the inherited screening outlines;
the fill color uses the original continuous SOVI_SCORE.
