Review date: 2026-10-15. These are isolated experimental copies, not approved
production replacements. The original 7.2 x 9.0-inch Figure 7 is the size
reference; D and every comparison map retain its original physical footprint.

PDF-derived previews were viewed, including all legend and comparison pages.
Normal, grayscale, protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia renders were reviewed
in the accessibility comparison and individual native-size proofs. This is
visual QA, not an observer experiment, calibrated physical printer proof or
clinical certification of color discriminability.

## Findings

- The blue-amber direction conveys increasing continuous SOVI without the
  original four abrupt hue-family changes. Increasing deficit consistently
  darkens every SOVI level; no family is assigned an arbitrary brighter burden.
- All four combinations are distinguishable in normal full color. Equal-SOVI
  burden comparisons are easier than precise independent reading of two values
  in a tiny polygon. Use the continuous key or exact CSV, not visual estimates,
  for numerical comparisons.
- Blue-amber retains a more useful chromatic direction under red-green stress
  tests than teal-wine. Distinctions still depend on burden and display size.
  Neither palette is universally accessibility-safe.
- In grayscale the burden coordinate remains ordered but continuous SOVI is
  lost. The original also loses vulnerability-category hues. Separate maps are
  the preferred reference when both variables must be readable without color.
- The inherited 0.62-pt high-high outlines remain clearly visible; they can
  dominate individual very small tracts, but were not thinned or redrawn.
  Ordinary polygon boundaries and white N/A fills/dark boundaries are unchanged.
- New D text has a 7.5-pt minimum at native size. No clipped labels, overlapping
  key entries or text outside the page was found. The 94-tract outline legend
  is separate from N/A and does not imply significance or intervention ranking.
- All seven output PDF pages contain vector geography and embedded fonts;
  there are zero raster image objects. The continuous PDF legend is function
  shading, not a rasterized map or a classified bivariate grid.
- Native display support is limited: 2,017 eligible tracts have polygon area
  below 1 mm2 and 700 below 0.25 mm2. Enlarged proofs make these easier to
  inspect but do not remove that publication-size limitation.

## Preservation and limitation

A-C pixel differences are zero at 150, 300 and 600 dpi for both palettes.
All 2,413 D polygon/boundary paths have unchanged vertices in actual output
inspection. The only intended scientific-map change is the positive-population
tract fill mapping; legend geometry/text changes are confined to D.

One pre-existing outline/GPKG envelope mismatch is retained and documented in
DATA_AND_GEOMETRY_QA.json. No geographic repair, new statistical test, refit,
manuscript update or publication-suite replacement was performed. Author
approval and a final journal-specific color/print proof remain necessary for
any future substitution.
