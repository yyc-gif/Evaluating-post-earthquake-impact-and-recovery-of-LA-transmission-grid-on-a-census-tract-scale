# Diagnostic Figure Caption

Mean conditional planning loss for each initialization (left) and paired mean differences (right). Positive differences favor Impact-seeded. Bars are five-endpoint Bonferroni95% paired t intervals over optimizer seeds, not physical-realization uncertainty. Solid lines use twenty paired seeds at 20k/50k/100k; dashed lines and hollow points use the first ten prospectively reserved trajectories at 100k/250k/500k. The extra hollow 100k point has no additional confidence bar and is descriptive, not a new primary endpoint. Different cohort means must not be read as within-seed marginal gains; those are separately tabulated. The gray band is the prospective +/-0.001h numerical tolerance, not a proven physical policy-benefit threshold. The frozen selected-GA horizontal reference is a development-selected sequence, not an expected optimizer score.
