# Manuscript-impact assessment: no formal update

## What the new evidence warrants

Do not describe K3 as the statistically validated or uniquely reproducible
number of community types. It remains scientifically interpretable, but new
random-split, bootstrap and geographic-transfer results do not substantiate
that stronger claim. Nor do they establish K4 or K5 as the replacement.

The recommended scientific revision, subject to author approval, is to frame
clustering as a descriptive discretization of continuous, scenario-conditioned
recovery and community attributes. Prioritize continuous service-deficit,
constraint-burden, population and SOVI relationships in substantive claims.

## Options requiring an author decision

1. Retain K3 for continuity and its baseline-versus-constraint interpretation,
   but explicitly disclose weak prediction strength, class-level dissolution,
   geographic sensitivity and that K was a parsimonious presentation choice.
   Do not claim this independent validation confirms K3.
2. Use K2 as a simpler categorical illustration: it has the strongest IID
   split/bootstrap support, but loses the baseline-versus-constraint subdivision
   and has weak geographic transfer. It would require a coordinated replacement
   of memberships, class descriptions, maps, profiles, captions and registry.
   This task does NOT execute that replacement.
3. Reduce the role of clustering in the main narrative and move multi-K
   descriptions to supplementary evidence. This best reflects the lack of
   agreement among validity methods without discarding interpretable patterns.

Not recommended: switch to K4 solely to expose the 37-tract proxy group, or
to K5 solely for maximum silhouette. The rare group is not rejected for being
small; its model dependence and resampling behavior are explicitly measured.

## Proposed wording, not inserted

"We used weighted K-means as a descriptive partition of tract-level initial
service, network dependency, recovery burden and community context. Independent
validation did not identify a unique clustering resolution: uniform-reference
Gap rules favored no additional partition, random-split prediction strength
favored a coarse two-group representation, and higher-resolution solutions
were sensitive to resampling and geography. We therefore interpret the
partitions as conditional summaries rather than naturally discrete community
regimes. Continuous recovery and vulnerability patterns remain the primary
quantitative evidence."

If K3 is retained, add: "Three groups were retained for their interpretable
separation of baseline and additional restoration burden, not because three
was independently established as an optimal number of types."

## Unchanged scope

RQ1/RQ2 physical results, five policy strategies, 1,000 realizations, social
quartiles, population, GA, official Figure 7/S8 and the October 14 manuscript
are not modified. NCV14 remains outside the fitted matrix. No inference about
actual delivered power or physical resilience is newly validated here.

Any later adoption needs an explicit author decision about the strength and
placement of categorical claims, plus coordinated scientific registry,
manuscript and figure updates on a separate approved integration task. This
branch is not to be merged automatically.
