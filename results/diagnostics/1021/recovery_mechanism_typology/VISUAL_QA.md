inspected at their7.2-inch publication width, not only as plotting canvases.
Corresponding300dpi PNG exports are supplied. No official artwork was opened
for editing or regenerated.

Titles, axes, legends and inset labels are inside page bounds. No clipping or
unreadable overlaps were found. The updated resource figure's two-row legend
was re-rendered and inspected after adding explicit continuous-model keys.
Figures are vector paths with embedded text, rather than rasterized charts.
All pages carry an exploratory/not-publication label. Captions explaining
training versus unused conditions and the limits of group interpretation are
in `README.md`; they are not pasted beneath the analytical panels.

These pages are diagnostic evidence, not replacements for Figure7 or a
declaration that K2/K3 are physical community types. The underlying original-unit
and all-K machine-readable results, including unfavorable cases, are retained.
