## Time quantities are not interchangeable

The old Stage 5 table is generated by
src/la_grid/revision/formal/stage7.py:materialize_formal_stage7_inputs. It first
forms mean service across 1,000 direct-community realizations, then finds the
first crossing of 0.8. That is T80 OF THE MEAN trajectory, not the mean or
median of realization-specific T80. Applicable direct-community and Impact-first
integrals were verified identical in earlier frozen audits.

This follow-up instead reads each already-saved event series, linearly projects
its saved service states to tracts and records the first exact event time at
availability >=0.8. A nonreaching realization remains NaN plus an explicit
unreached fraction. No missing threshold time is assigned 480 h. All 6,897,000
tract-realization threshold values in the three selected policy cases are
reached within the horizon; the unreached outcome is constant zero and therefore
not discriminatory. The median/P10/P90 are realization summaries, not CIs.

The primary temporal response is each tract's realization-median T80. A plotted
class point averages those tract medians; its bars span P10-P90 ACROSS TRACTS.
It is not an aggregate population recovery time or a pooled realization median.

Early, intermediate and late deficits integrate [0,24], [24,72] and [72,480]
using exact saved step-event overlaps. They sum to the existing 480-hour
integral. The late segment is very small in these cases; it does not meet a
5-h between-group contrast merely because it is listed as a separate outcome.

## Independence hierarchy

Random is not among the five policies forming MeanDelta, but shares hazard,
damage, network, mapping, source assumptions and repair-duration samples.
Its evidence is policy-transfer support within the model, not external validation.
Other resources share physical samples; other earthquake scenarios change those
samples but retain the same modeling framework. Realization-level time and
window summaries share the underlying trajectories with fitted recovery burden.
Window sums and source-derived quantities must not be counted as independent
validation votes. Unconstrained total burden is a fitted coordinate and is
explicitly EXCLUDED from the evaluated outcome list.

No empirical customer-outage, delivered-MW, clinical-service or repair-time
observation is available here for independent field validation. Nor do the new
comparisons remove Vulnerability-first's procedural relationship with frozen
SOVI Q4 in the fitted ensemble.

## Reproducibility boundary

Derived tract outcomes, realization-specific T80, all subsampling labels,
training indices, benchmark cutpoints and complete scores are committed through
Git LFS. They permit reproduction of the statistical analysis from Git alone.
Source-level event extraction additionally requires the manifest-bound local
archive, registered in FINAL_REVISION_RUN_SEQUENCE/EXTERNAL_ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json.
It is not promised by a clean Git clone. LA_GRID_FROZEN_SOURCE_ROOT can point to
an exact, hash-matching copy; absent sources must produce a failure, not a silent
substitution. The raw saved station states are never overwritten.
