# Proposed wording only: K3 remains optional

## Scientific position

The follow-up finds engineering content in the distinction between baseline
recovery difficulty and sensitivity to limited restoration resources. It does
not establish a reproducible three-class taxonomy or a material prediction
advantage over simpler/continuous descriptions. Do not describe this as a
successful independent external validation of K3.

## Methods, if a descriptive K3 is retained

"We used a three-group partition as a descriptive summary of the seven
registered tract attributes, rather than as evidence of naturally discrete
community regimes. We examined unfitted temporal summaries, an excluded Random
restoration priority, and previously simulated resource and earthquake cases.
All comparisons used saved model outputs. Repeated 70% and 85% subsampling
assessed membership and recovery-profile persistence; simple quantile groups
and continuous seven-input models provided benchmarks. These evaluations
assess model-conditioned descriptive usefulness, not external outage validity."

## Results

"The three-profile representation distinguished tracts with greater baseline
burden from those more sensitive to reduced restoration resources. These
differences did not imply a universal ranking of community recovery: the
resource-sensitive profile did not have the greatest burden under the excluded
Random priority. Two large class cores were relatively persistent, whereas
membership of the intermediate profile was unstable. Prediction gains over
two groups were small, and continuous descriptions captured the unfitted
recovery outcomes more accurately. The classes are therefore interpreted as
optional descriptive summaries rather than stable community types."

## Limitation

"The validation outcomes share the frozen network, station-to-tract mapping,
source proxies and simulation assumptions. Some also share physical samples
and restoration inputs with the fitted attributes. Agreement across these
outcomes is supporting internal evidence, not independent empirical validation.
The Q-linked initial-state exception and policy-set dependence further limit
interpretation of the categorical representation."

Do not claim that a 428-tract resource-sensitive class was reproduced in full,
that the 37-tract source-proxy group is physically resilient, or that a larger
subsample uniformly resolves the earlier stability problems. No wording above
has been inserted into the official manuscript or captions.
