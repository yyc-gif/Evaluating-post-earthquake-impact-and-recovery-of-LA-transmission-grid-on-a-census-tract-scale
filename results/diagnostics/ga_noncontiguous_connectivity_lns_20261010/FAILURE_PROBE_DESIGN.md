# Prospective bounded proposal-mechanism probe

This is registered during the confirmation batch after its first completed
cases, before executing these probes. It is adaptive diagnosis, not a second
confirmatory optimizer comparison or hyperparameter selection. The confirmation
algorithm and outcomes remain unmodified.

For each fixed parent (shared older prior, frozen GA, refined ILS), seeds
1400..1423 choose an all-64 connectivity bundle and a uniform damaged bundle
of equal size. Compare the committed noncontiguous insertion beam against
joint independent slot swaps. Swaps avoid shifting every intervening first-wave
priority. Swaps receive the same attempted candidate effort as insertion,
including repeated chromosomes, not free additional search. A best-so-far
parent is included in both; report strict improvements separately from ties.
Exact fitness remains all 64 states with original objective.

Record proposed/retained fitness, attempted/distinct calls, moved ranks,
first-wave crew changes and station completion changes across all 64 states.
For each retained proposal compute sample15 and mean64 changes. The prototype
swaps choose each member's original, favorable crew slot, zero, or uniform
random target; no station is forced into a block. One macro never updates its
parent. Twenty-four seeds are repeated proposal draws, not independent physical
samples or 100k optimizations. No inference of optimizer superiority follows.

Separate timing/slack audit asks whether high source-wait weights mostly reflect
irreducible repair durations rather than postponement by the scheduler. The
relaxed independent-origin benchmark is descriptive, not a feasible schedule
or a certified bound. Complete negative outcomes and candidate hashes retained.
