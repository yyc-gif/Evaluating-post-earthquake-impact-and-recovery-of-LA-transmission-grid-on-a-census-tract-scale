# Validation Figure Review

The frozen exporter produced RECOVERY.png and DISPARITY.png after all 2,000
shared realizations completed. Both actual 1,620 x 900-pixel files were
visually reviewed at their rendered size. Axes, units, legends and labels
are visible and unclipped. Four fixed conditions have consistent identifiers
and colors across the two figures.

The full 0-480 h recovery plot intentionally keeps the frozen horizon. Its
curves overlap substantially, so detailed effect claims must use the paired
tables rather than apparent visual separation. No plotting parameters or
frozen figure bytes were changed during review. These are diagnostic
validation figures, not replacements for manuscript artwork.

DISPARITY.png shows mean signed Q4-minus-Q1 **service availability**, not
absolute disparity or cumulative loss. The larger GA peak cannot be
described as an inequality reduction. The absolute-loss gap and Gini are
reported separately in SCIENTIFIC_INTERPRETATION.md and the numerical tables.

Original exporter hashes are in fixed_2000_20261010/EXPORT_RECEIPT.json;
the same files are also stored losslessly inside FIXED_2000_FULL_ARCHIVE.zip.
