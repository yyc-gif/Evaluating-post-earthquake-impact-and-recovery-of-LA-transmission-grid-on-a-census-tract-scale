## Reviewer Implications: Project-Specific Inference

The primary examples support an established search family and explicit separation of priority representation from physical scheduling. They do not require a hybrid, a newly named algorithm, or many operators. The manuscript must explain the actual decoder, feasible task/crew behavior, objective, initialization, acceptance, perturbation and stopping rules. We must disclose empirical calibration and uncertainty, complete permutations/hashes, independent search seeds, matched computational work and independent production-path parity. A decoder-based solution is feasible under the modeled constraints, not evidence of utility-level feasibility under omitted electrical or operational constraints.

Transpositions generate the full permutation group, which makes swap a complete generating operation. That algebraic fact does not make strict descent globally convergent; stagnation-triggered compound swaps are an engineering diversification rule. Historical neighborhood tests and new repeated Impact-only runs supply application-specific evidence of adequacy, not a mathematical optimality guarantee.

The 64 planning realizations are already development data. Optimizer-seed intervals, selected-sequence sample diagnostics and previously frozen validation concern different uncertainties. No precedent licenses reuse of inspected validation outcomes for new candidate selection. Source-specific physical assumptions and objective differences remain explicit.
