No algorithm, seed, budget or physical sample was changed in response to engineering smoke outcomes. The main search implementation stayed byte-identical throughout computation. Analysis/reporting additions do not alter search mechanics or objective authority.

Later supplemental checks added a synthetic 92-station replay and a synthetic late-restoration horizon case; the four targeted test modules passed all 26 checks twice. The completed CSVs and native event traces were reused for finalization after casting the count to Python `int`; no physical evaluation was repeated.
