The seven process-chain figure candidates and the GA reproducibility candidate are generated from the frozen sources listed in `FIGURE_V2_PANEL_CROSSWALK.csv` and `FIGURE_V2_SOURCE_HASHES.csv`. Read `STORY_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md` before interpreting coverage. Candidate numbering is temporary and does not imply Main/Supplement selection.
