Definitions, uncertainty and evidence boundaries

Mean and 5th–95th realization range describe the saved damage/recovery ensemble; they are not confidence intervals. The saved-interval tab shows only the already computed bootstrap CIs. distributional effects subtraction joins the same realization IDs first. No fresh resampling is performed here.

Signed Q4−Q1 and mean realization-wise |Q4−Q1| are distinct. Population-weighted Gini: 0 = equal tract service loss; larger = more unequal service loss. These metrics need not move in the same direction; none alone determines fairness.

Historical hazards and 2pc50 use different adopted fragility parameterizations, so their differences are not pure PGA sensitivity. Resource and duration choices are discrete tested conditions, not a continuous response curve.

Missing/unreached observations remain missing. Unconstrained results exist only for the baseline duration/resource cases. No alternative vulnerability-definition policy was re-executed. Legacy ±1 h population categories are disclosed separately and are not used by current maps.
