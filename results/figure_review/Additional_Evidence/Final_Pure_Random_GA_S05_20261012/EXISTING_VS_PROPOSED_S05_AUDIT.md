## Layout And Panel Decisions

`S05_Existing_vs_Proposed_REVIEW.pdf` places the original and proposed vector PDFs side by side at exactly 185 mm each, on a 405x240 mm review sheet. It is not a new manuscript figure. The proposed four-panel page is 185x178 mm with embedded Arial and minimum 7.5 pt text; a 600-dpi PNG is supplied.

- A: right-continuous observed archives and their pointwise median; no interpolated fitness observations, no generation-average fitness, no confidence band.
- B: unjittered paired initial/final losses; selected-seed diamonds; same absolute hour scale as A. Close final values overlap honestly. Exact seed-level CSV and caption report the range instead of magnifying hundredths of an hour.
- C: complete empirical CDF, including both tails (-4.967486 to +1.139627 h). The small explicitly labeled inset depicts only saved mean inference; its focused interval scale must not be read as the physical effect distribution.
- D: signed bars from zero; red increased loss, blue decreased loss, dark net. All components use the same hour scale and native same-cohort data. Tiny threshold loss is not artificially amplified.

Four panels fit at publication width after moving reliability disclosures below B and separating D labels from its x-axis. No alternative omitted-evidence layout was needed. This remains an author-review candidate, not an official replacement. Review concerns are methodological qualifications, not unresolved clipping or font defects.
