# Figure S05. Random-initialization search and independent validation of the frozen GA sequence

(A) Best-observed planning loss versus distinct objective evaluations for 20 independent pure-random genetic algorithm (GA) runs on 64 fixed 2pc50 planning realizations. Each population begins with 100 independent random station permutations and each run ends at 100,000 distinct evaluations. Pale step curves are saved individual archives; the dark step curve is their pointwise median, not generation-average fitness. Impact-first is a reference only, not an inserted chromosome. (B) Paired initial-population and final best losses for all 20 seeds, on the same absolute-loss scale as A. Diamonds identify selected seed 2026103108 (32.995522 h). Close final outcomes overlap without jitter. Pure-random initialization was a post-data methodological choice: only 10/20 runs met the earlier strict proximity threshold, failing the registered 90% single-run criterion. Finite-budget selection does not establish global optimality or guaranteed single-run convergence.

(C) Empirical cumulative distribution of all 2,000 paired GA-minus-Impact-first population-weighted normalized service-loss differences in the separate, previously completed fresh physical-validation cohort. Negative differences favor GA; 1,741 realizations (87.05%) improve and 259 worsen. Zero and paired-mean references are distinct. The inset reports the mean difference, -0.532675 h, with its saved simultaneous 95% confidence interval [-0.563982, -0.502321] h, retaining the original Bonferroni two-contrast family; this is uncertainty in the mean, not a physical-outcome quantile range. (D) Signed mean accounting contributions from that same cohort: self loss +0.277475 h, threshold loss +0.010638 h, source-path loss -0.820788 h, and net -0.532675 h. Source-path reduction dominates, partly offset by the other components; these are accounting terms, not identified station-level causal effects.

Validation integrates normalized modeled service deficit over 0-480 h under the unchanged population mapping and Core-source gate. The validated legacy last-position elitism candidate is used throughout, not the later revised-elitism candidate. Lower average population burden does not establish better equity: mean absolute SOVI Q4-Q1 burden disparity increases by 0.232733 h and population-weighted tract Gini by 0.009790. Modeled service is not delivered electricity or actual customer restoration.
