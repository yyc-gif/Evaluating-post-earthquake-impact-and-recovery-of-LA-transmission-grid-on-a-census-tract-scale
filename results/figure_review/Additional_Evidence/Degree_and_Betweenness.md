Degree and betweenness in the study network. A/B show the saved station centrality values on the common study-area extent; C/D show their distributions over the modeled substations. Degree counts neighboring substations. Betweenness describes intermediary position on shortest network paths. These static metrics define two infrastructure priorities; neither is a direct measure of delivered electricity or a validated optimal repair order.
