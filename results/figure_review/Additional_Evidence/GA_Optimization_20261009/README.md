# GA optimization review evidence

Open [GA_OPTIMIZATION_REVIEW_PACKET.pdf](GA_OPTIMIZATION_REVIEW_PACKET.pdf) for all seven artworks at native 185-mm width, each followed by its caption.

- FigS05_Optimization_Candidate: equal-budget progress, survival/population interaction, original/tuned/local/hybrid searches and fixed-candidate cohort effects.
- GA_Parameter_Interactions: controlled mutation, selection, elitism and crossover interactions.
- GA_Original_Population_Dynamics: reproductive population versus independent archive, diversity and observed selection differential.
- GA_Convergence_and_Computation: distinct queries, attempted calls, elapsed time and generations; includes the 500,000-query comparison.
- GA_Operator_Comparison: nine separately labeled genetic/replacement variants.
- Restoration_With_GA_Review_Candidate: recovery curves and community outcomes with a planning-selected GA comparator.
- GA_Network_Station_Community_Mechanism: station functionality, source-connected availability and component/community loss.

These are diagnostic review candidates. Current numbered Main/Supplement artwork and formal strategy results are preserved. No candidate is promoted here. The 1,000-realization cohort has been inspected previously and is explicitly reused for exploratory assessment.
