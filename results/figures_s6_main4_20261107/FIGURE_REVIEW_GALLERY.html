Figure 4. Network-dependent source connectivity during recovery

Vector PDF

(A) Recovery of population-dependency-weighted joint functionality and source connection through the full network (solid) or a fixed precomputed path (dashed). (B) Their difference shows restoration-sequence-dependent alternative-route support. (C) Station source access conditional on station functionality; (D) additional conditional reliability beyond the fixed path. Triangles mark Core sources. A/B are dynamic recovery comparisons; C/D are fragility-based station diagnostics. These topological measures are not delivered electricity.

Figure 8. Community recovery types and social vulnerability

Vector PDF

A shows six feature distributions; B shows standardized means for all seven fitted variables, grouped by research question; C shows P2/K3 geography. D maps continuous SOVI and five-policy mean cumulative service deficit; outlines mark frozen Q4 and the cohort P75. The descriptive intersection contains 94 tracts (350,799 residents); high-burden prevalence is 16.23% in Q4 and 27.97% outside Q4. This policy-conditioned screen is not a significance test or intervention-priority score.
