## Visual policy

Retain embedded Arial, restrained headings, white backgrounds, quantitative scale ordering and established scientific colors. Do not harmonize unlike variables by repainting every plot. No scientific panel is removed to meet a figure limit. Compactness must come from reflow/spacing, not making already small text smaller.

Final derivative, accessibility and packet inspection results are in `FINAL_VISUAL_INSPECTION.md`. No portal or journal-wide acceptance is inferred from this inspection.
