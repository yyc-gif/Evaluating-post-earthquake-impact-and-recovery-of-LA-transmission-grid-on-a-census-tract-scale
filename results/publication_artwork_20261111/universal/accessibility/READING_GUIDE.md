# Reading the community-context map

Master Figure 8D uses tract-level values, not cluster-average values. Its horizontal legend dimension is cumulative modeled service deficit in equivalent hours; darker values indicate greater burden. Its blue-to-amber dimension represents the continuous social-vulnerability score on the original 0-100 scale. These two dimensions are not a new composite risk index.

The thin outlines identify the original highest vulnerability quartile together with burden at or above the fixed cohort 75th percentile (38.38135467381475 equivalent hours). The frozen screen contains 94 tracts and 350,799 represented residents. This is descriptive co-occurrence, not a significance test, causal effect or repair-priority ranking. White polygons with charcoal borders are the 16 zero-population N/A tracts, not observations of zero burden.

Grayscale cannot retain both independent color dimensions. Use the [aligned single-variable reference](aligned_sovi_deficit.pdf) to read vulnerability and recovery burden separately. Its historical P75 abbreviation means the same cohort percentile, not a separate score. The source and exact payload hash are registered in `../../qa/ACCESSIBILITY_REFERENCE.json`.

Panels A-C describe the existing three community types using seven fitted variables. Their ECDF line styles and printed profile values provide non-hue cues. The class map still relies on categorical color and is not universally distinguishable in every grayscale or color-vision condition. Class-feature contrasts describe the fitted partition; they are not independent empirical validation of discrete types.

Throughout the collection, modeled service availability, integrated deficit and recovery time are distinct quantities. None is an observation of delivered customer electricity. Source-connected graph topology is an intermediate model state, not power flow. Accessible descriptions and companion views do not change these definitions or the frozen numerical results.
