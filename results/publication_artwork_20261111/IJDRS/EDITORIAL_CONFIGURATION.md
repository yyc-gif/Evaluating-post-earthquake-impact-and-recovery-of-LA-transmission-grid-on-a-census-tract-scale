## Information proposed for supplementary presentation

The mapping/assignment comparison (master Figure 2) supports methodological credibility but can be read after the workflow without interrupting the main mechanism sequence. Resource effects (master Figure 7) test the dependence of the baseline contrasts and must remain discussed and cited, even if their full panels move to supplementary presentation. Community types and the direct burden/vulnerability map (master Figure 8) are descriptive context; their seven fitted variables do not externally validate discrete types. These three complete figures join the existing eight supplementary figures in an **eleven-artwork proposal**.

The linked IJDRS instructions do not establish an ESM allowance or maximum. The journal has published supplementary files, but that does not authorize this particular count. The eleven-page figure proposal plus captions is prepared for editorial review, not mislabeled as an accepted appendix. No information is silently deleted and no supplementary slot is assumed unlimited.

The current author-controlled manuscript must settle that emphasis before submission.
