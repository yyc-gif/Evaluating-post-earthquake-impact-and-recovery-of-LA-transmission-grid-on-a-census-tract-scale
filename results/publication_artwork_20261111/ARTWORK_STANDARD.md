# Journal-neutral artwork standard

Scientific authority comes first: numerical values, uncertainty definitions, geometric coordinates and model meaning must remain traceable to the accepted sources. A smaller file or a successful export is not evidence of equivalence.

The complete collection keeps its eight main and eight supplementary scientific identities. Selection and numbering for a journal are configuration data, not edits to the scientific masters. Original resources and code remain available even when a delivery PDF embeds font subsets.

## Design and print size

- Retain Arial regular/bold, restrained headings, white backgrounds and the existing semantic palettes. Travel, repair, damage states, strategies, community classes and continuous burden are distinct concepts; they are not forced into one palette.
- Inspect native-size maps, curves, uncertainty bars, legends and labels. Printed lettering matters more than DPI metadata. Existing 7-7.5-pt details are recorded as a portability caution; larger 8-12-pt lettering is preferable when space permits, but is not claimed as an IJDRS-specific requirement.
- Do not uniformly shrink dense multi-panel artwork to a single column. The export guard reports effective font size. Illustrative 185-, 174- and 85-mm placement checks are not journal mandates.
- The compact workflow removes measured empty horizontal strips, retaining frame alignment and connectors. No map, schedule, event-state matrix or statistical graphic is rescaled. Its ten smallest labels are increased from 7 to 7.5 pt. The full-height original remains authoritative and recoverable.
- Compact PDF strips omit only Form calls outside conservative ancestor/BBox bounds. Unmodified leaf artwork is reused; exact decimal unit translations avoid unintended scaling. This also prevents the excessive rendering memory of repeatedly importing an entire GIS page. Reflow can change finite-precision edge antialiasing, so its measured pixel differences are reported separately from the pixel-identical lossless delivery PDFs. At 600 dpi, differences must remain within existing one-pixel edge neighborhoods (any nonzero local contrast), apart from one-level quantization, with channel/fraction/mean bounds in the QA record. No broad interior or scientific-value change is accepted.
- Keep captions out of individual production image files. Review packets pair each figure with its caption without pretending to be the journal's final typeset pages.

## Technical conversion

- Preserve vector drawing streams. Try resource pruning, identical font-program sharing, font subsetting and lossless object/Flate compression; accept optimized delivery PDFs only after exact text, drawing-stream and 150/600-dpi pixel checks.
- Do not simplify tract polygons, round scientific values, downsample the entire page, convert vector charts to JPEG, or treat 10 MB as a universal acceptance threshold.
- Keep the original complete-resource PDF as an editing source. Subsetting retains the used glyphs; adding new text may require the original font/code.
- TIFF derivatives use lossless LZW, RGB, 600 dpi at declared physical dimensions, with a verified pixel round trip. The output uses an sRGB interpretation; this is not a calibrated CMYK print proof. The 300/600/1200-dpi export settings serve different content and production requests; one setting is not universally sufficient.
- PNG is a lossless sharing/preview format. JPEG is deliberately not generated for these text-and-line-rich scientific figures. A journal requiring JPEG needs a separately evaluated derivative.
- EPS is not claimed without an appropriate converter and round-trip inspection, especially for transparency. The SVG trial exceeded its appearance limits and is rejected rather than mislabeled as faithful production artwork. Outlined lettering also loses text editability and accessibility semantics; PDF/code/captions remain the primary reusable record.

## Accessibility

Preserve quantitative ordering and do not silently change established color meanings. Retain labels, line styles and marker identities where already meaningful. CVD simulations are diagnostic approximations, not universal accessibility certificates. Dense strategy curves can still overlap, and a continuous bivariate map cannot preserve two independent color dimensions in grayscale. The restored aligned single-variable companion and descriptive captions remain necessary alternatives. No new risk or hotspot score is constructed.
