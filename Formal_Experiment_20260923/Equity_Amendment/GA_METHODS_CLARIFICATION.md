# Methods-ready optimization and planning description

The July submitted permutation GA maximized a station-level surrogate: weighted early station completion (population, hospital, vulnerability, and network importance) less a makespan penalty. The candidate's fitness did not execute source-gated station recovery, tract dependency mapping, or tract cumulative burden. Consequently its surrogate score and the subsequently reported tract T80/AUC need not order strategies the same way. Fitness values under different objective definitions must never be compared numerically.

The revised permutation GA keeps population 100, 100 fixed generations, ordered crossover probability 0.8, inversion mutation probability 0.2, tournament selection of size 3, and independent seeds 42–46. The change is the evaluator and the retained-candidate rule. A 92-station permutation is filtered to DS>0 separately in each planning realization. The retained C57 event scheduler uses that realization's positive repair durations and directed road times; completion events update functionality, the retained 14-source/0.5 gate propagates state, and the production utility-compatible tract mapping produces an event-exact cumulative service deficit. Let B_r(pi) be the population-times-resolved-mass weighted community burden under sequence pi in planning realization r. The fitness is `-(1/64) sum_{r=1}^{64} B_r(pi)`. This optimizes a modeled *aggregate community-efficiency* outcome, not tract disparity, Q4 burden, actual electricity delivery, or a global optimum.

All 64 planning physical realizations use the same fixed 2pc50 station PGA field. Independent, predeclared SeedSequence streams draw station damage state from the July fragility probabilities and one positive DS-specific repair duration for each damaged station. These are 64 possible damage/workload realizations conditional on one hazard field, not 64 independently sampled PGA maps. They are disjoint from the 1,000 2pc50 evaluation physical realizations. Within a realization, every candidate sees identical DS, duration, graph, travel, crew, gate, and mapping inputs.

Before GA search, the seven fixed rule-based sequences were evaluated on the *same* 64 planning realizations and *same* direct objective. Their best, impact-first, is the deterministic incumbent: the best known fixed rule before search begins. A best-so-far archive compares each generation's candidate with this incumbent. Across all five seeds and the fixed budget, no searched candidate exceeded the incumbent fitness `-33.57830255999965`; the retained `direct-community` sequence is therefore exactly impact-first. It must not be presented as an independent GA improvement. This limited-budget result does not establish global optimality.

The newly frozen `vulnerability-first` comparator is distinct: a deterministic 92-station order based on mapped population in the pre-existing highest NRI-derived `SOVI_SCORE` quartile, with total mapped population and station ID tie-breaks. It is explicitly equity-informed targeting, but it does not optimize disparity or guarantee equity improvement. Its evaluation uses only frozen evaluation physical samples; no new search or planning sample was used.
