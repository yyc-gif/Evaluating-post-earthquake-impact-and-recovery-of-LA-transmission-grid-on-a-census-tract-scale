# Frozen equity-informed policy amendment

**Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_EQUITY_POLICY_EXECUTION`.** The machine-readable authority is `EQUITY_POLICY_AMENDMENT.json`. This amendment adds exactly one deterministic comparator to the unchanged `JULY92_REVIEWER_REVISION_FINAL_V1` experiment. It is motivated by Reviewer 2 #14's request to show whether targeting vulnerable communities changes their absolute recovery burden, disparity, system costs, and who benefits or is delayed. The retained `direct-community` strategy minimizes aggregate population burden and is not an equity optimizer.

The policy uses the **existing formal NRI-derived `SOVI_SCORE` quartile assignment** in `R1_Comment1_July92_Utility_Constraint/MAPPING_STRUCTURE_SENSITIVITY_TRACTS.csv`; Q4 is the highest-vulnerability quartile. For each of the retained 92 stations, set `V_s = sum_{tract r in Q4} Population_r * W_M1[r,s]`, with the frozen production `JULY_UTILITY_CONSTRAINED_92` weights. Rank all 92 station IDs once by `V_s` descending, total mapped population `P_s = sum_r Population_r * W_M1[r,s]` descending, then station ID ascending. No hospital, topology, GA, or tuned coefficient enters this policy. At execution, delete only DS0 stations and retain the remaining relative order. A station with zero Q4 mass remains in the full sequence under the declared tie-breaks.

The new policy consumes exactly the already frozen 1,000 evaluation physical samples in each of Northridge, SanFernando, LongBeach, and 2pc50 under C57/D1, plus the same 2pc50 samples under six already frozen nonbaseline OFAT cases: C29, C86, C114, duration ×0.75, ×1.25, and ×1.5. Maximum additional trajectories: **10,000**. No original trajectory, sample, rule sequence, direct-community sequence, graph, 14-source set, production gate, M1 mapping, or 480 h common horizon is changed.

Predeclared contrasts use hospital-first and impact-first separately. Report population burden and T50/T80, hospital-tract burden, Q1–Q4 absolute burden, signed and absolute Q4−Q1 gaps, Gini, and paired tract/population improvements and deteriorations. The ±1 h classification threshold is practical, not a significance test. Use one realization as the paired statistical unit. No favorable result is required for retaining the comparator. The amendment is an explicitly **equity-informed targeting rule**, not a proof of equity improvement, a new GA, or an empirical service share.
